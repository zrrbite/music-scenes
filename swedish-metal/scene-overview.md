# Scene Overview: Timeline

## Stockholm death metal (1987–1993)
| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1987 | Nihilist (later [Entombed](bands/entombed.md)) formed |
| 1988 | [Dismember](bands/dismember.md) formed |
| 1989 | Nihilist becomes Entombed |
| 1990 | Entombed's *Left Hand Path*, recorded at Sunlight Studio, defines the HM-2 "buzzsaw" sound. [Opeth](bands/opeth.md) formed |
| 1991 | Dismember's *Like an Ever Flowing Stream*, Entombed's *Clandestine*. [Katatonia](bands/katatonia.md) formed |
| 1993 | Entombed's *Wolverine Blues* starts "death 'n' roll" |

## Gothenburg melodic death metal (1989–2000)
| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1989 | Septic Broiler (later [Dark Tranquillity](bands/dark-tranquillity.md)) formed |
| 1990 | [At the Gates](bands/at-the-gates.md) and [In Flames](bands/in-flames.md) formed |
| 1992 | At the Gates' *The Red in the Sky Is Ours* |
| 1993 | Dark Tranquillity's *Skydancer* |
| 1995 | *Slaughter of the Soul* and *The Gallery*, the year the Gothenburg sound peaks |
| 1996 | *The Jester Race*. At the Gates split; the Björlers form The Haunted |
| 1999–2000 | In Flames' *Colony* and *Clayman* take the sound to a wide audience |

## Meshuggah and the progressive line (1987–now)
| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1987 | [Meshuggah](bands/meshuggah.md) formed in Umeå |
| 1995 | *Destroy Erase Improve* introduces Meshuggah's polyrhythmic style |
| 1996 | Katatonia's *Brave Murder Day* (Åkerfeldt on vocals) |
| 2001 | Opeth's *Blackwater Park* |
| 2005 | [Vildhjarta](bands/vildhjarta.md) formed |
| 2008 | Meshuggah's *obZen*; "djent" becomes a movement worldwide |
| 2009 | [Humanity's Last Breath](bands/humanitys-last-breath.md) formed **(verify)** |
| 2011 | Vildhjarta's *Måsstaden* (the start of "thall"). Opeth's *Heritage* drops growls |
| 2021 | *Måsstaden under vatten* and *Välde* |
| 2024 | Opeth's *The Last Will and Testament* brings growls back. [Mirar](bands/mirar.md)'s *Mare* EP |
| 2025 | Mirar's *Ascension* |
