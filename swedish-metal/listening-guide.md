# Listening Guide

## Coming from modern tech/prog death metal (Quebec, West Coast)
Start with the rhythmic lineage, which will feel most familiar:
1. Meshuggah – *obZen* (2008)
2. Vildhjarta – *Måsstaden* (2011)
3. Humanity's Last Breath – *Välde* (2021)
4. Opeth – *Blackwater Park* (2001)

## The essential overview
1. Entombed – *Left Hand Path* (1990): the Stockholm sound
2. At the Gates – *Slaughter of the Soul* (1995): the Gothenburg sound
3. Opeth – *Blackwater Park* (2001): progressive
4. Meshuggah – *obZen* (2008): the rhythmic revolution
5. Vildhjarta – *Måsstaden* (2011): where thall begins
6. Humanity's Last Breath – *Välde* (2021): the heaviest point

## Old-school buzzsaw
- Entombed – *Left Hand Path* (1990), *Clandestine* (1991)
- Dismember – *Like an Ever Flowing Stream* (1991)

## Melodic
- At the Gates – *Slaughter of the Soul* (1995)
- Dark Tranquillity – *The Gallery* (1995), *Damage Done* (2002)
- In Flames – *The Jester Race* (1996), *Clayman* (2000)

## Dark and melancholic
- Katatonia – *Brave Murder Day* (1996), *The Great Cold Distance* (2006)
- Opeth – *Damnation* (2003, entirely clean vocals)

## Heaviest
- Meshuggah – *Nothing* (2002) or *Koloss* (2012)
- Humanity's Last Breath – *Abyssal* (2019), *Välde* (2021)
