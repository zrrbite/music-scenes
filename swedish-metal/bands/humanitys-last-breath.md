# Humanity's Last Breath

- **Base:** Helsingborg
- **Formed:** around 2009 **(verify)**
- **Key members:** Buster Odeholm (guitar, production, founder), Filip Danielsson (vocals)
- **Style:** Thall / blackened deathcore: extremely low, dissonant and atmospheric

## History
Buster Odeholm started Humanity's Last Breath as a project and grew it into a full band.
Its self-titled debut (2013) was raw, crushing deathcore with thall elements. After a long
gap they signed to Unique Leader for *Abyssal* (2019), which brought a much darker, more
atmospheric sound and wider recognition.

*Välde* (2021) is widely considered their best: enormous, blackened and cinematic, and often
named as one of the heaviest records of its time. Odeholm's production work, including with
[Vildhjarta](vildhjarta.md), is a big part of the modern Swedish thall sound. A newer album,
*Ashen* (2024) **(verify)**, followed.
