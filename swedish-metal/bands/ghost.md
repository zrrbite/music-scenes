# Ghost

- **Base:** Linköping, Sweden
- **Formed:** 2006 (first releases 2010)
- **Key member:** Tobias Forge (vocals, songwriter), who appears as a series of "Papa Emeritus" anti-pope characters and later Papa V Perpetua; backed by masked, anonymous "Nameless Ghouls"
- **Style:** Theatrical occult rock / heavy metal with 70s rock and pop hooks

## History
Ghost combine satanic, theatrical imagery with catchy, melodic songs that owe more to Blue
Öyster Cult, ABBA and 70s hard rock than to extreme metal. The debut *Opus Eponymous* (2010) came
out on Lee Dorrian's Rise Above Records, the label at the center of the [doom](../../doom/README.md)
and retro rock underground.

*Infestissumam* (2013), *Meliora* (2015, with the Grammy-winning "Cirice"), *Prequelle* (2018, with
"Rats" and "Dance Macabre"), *Impera* (2022) and *Skeletá* (2025) made them one of the biggest metal
bands in the world. Tobias Forge's identity was kept secret until 2017, when a lawsuit by former
band members revealed it.
