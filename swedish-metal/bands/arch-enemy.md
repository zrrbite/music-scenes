# Arch Enemy

- **Base:** Halmstad, Sweden
- **Formed:** 1995
- **Key members:** Michael Amott (guitar, founder), Christopher Amott (guitar, on and off), Sharlee D'Angelo (bass), Daniel Erlandsson (drums); vocalists Johan Liiva (to 2000), Angela Gossow (2000–2014), Alissa White-Gluz (2014–2025 **(verify)**)

## History
Michael Amott had played guitar in Carnage and in Carcass, on the classic *Heartwork* (1993),
before founding Arch Enemy with his brother Christopher. Their style is melodic death metal built
on twin-guitar harmonies and big, classic-metal solos. *Black Earth* (1996) launched them.

Angela Gossow replaced Johan Liiva in 2000, and *Wages of Sin* (2001) made her one of the
best-known extreme metal vocalists in the world. *Doomsday Machine* (2005) followed. In 2014
Montreal-born Alissa White-Gluz, formerly of The Agonist, took over, which links the band to the
[Quebec](../../quebec/README.md) scene. *War Eternal* (2014), *Will to Power* (2017), *Deceivers*
(2022) and *Blood Dynasty* (2025) followed. Michael Amott also leads the stoner rock band
Spiritual Beggars.
