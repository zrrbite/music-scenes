# Dark Tranquillity

- **Base:** Gothenburg
- **Formed:** 1989 as Septic Broiler; renamed in 1990
- **Key members:** Mikael Stanne (vocals), Niklas Sundin (guitar, artwork; left 2020)

## History
One of the three founding bands of Gothenburg melodic death metal, with At the Gates and In
Flames. The debut *Skydancer* (1993) had Anders Fridén on vocals. He then swapped bands with
Mikael Stanne, who moved from rhythm guitar to vocals in Dark Tranquillity while Fridén
joined In Flames. *The Gallery* (1995) is their classic.

On *Projector* (1999) they experimented with clean vocals and piano. *Damage Done* (2002)
returned to a heavier sound, and the band has stayed consistent and active ever since.
Of the three founders, they're the one that never broke up or changed direction drastically.
