# Katatonia

- **Base:** Stockholm
- **Formed:** 1991
- **Key members:** Jonas Renkse (vocals, originally drums), Anders Nyström (guitar)

## History
Katatonia started as a doom/death band (*Dance of December Souls*, 1993). On *Brave Murder
Day* (1996), Renkse couldn't do harsh vocals, so his friend Mikael Åkerfeldt of [Opeth](opeth.md)
sang them. On *Discouraged Ones* (1998), Renkse switched to clean singing and the band moved
toward melancholic, atmospheric dark rock.

*Last Fair Deal Gone Down* (2001), *Viva Emptiness* (2003), *The Great Cold Distance* (2006) and
*Night Is the New Day* (2009) made them one of the defining bands of the "sad metal" sound.
They're a cousin to Opeth: different destination, similar journey.
