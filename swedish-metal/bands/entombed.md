# Entombed

- **Base:** Stockholm
- **Formed:** 1987 as Nihilist; renamed Entombed in 1989
- **Key members:** Lars-Göran "LG" Petrov (vocals, d. 2021), Uffe Cederlund (guitar), Alex Hellid (guitar), Nicke Andersson (drums)

## History
As Nihilist, the teenage members recorded demos that are now legendary in the Stockholm
underground. They renamed themselves Entombed and released *Left Hand Path* (1990) on
Earache. Recorded at Sunlight Studio with Tomas Skogsberg, its HM-2 guitar tone became the
signature of Swedish death metal. *Clandestine* (1991) followed, with drummer Nicke Andersson
also handling vocals on the record.

*Wolverine Blues* (1993) moved toward a groovier, rock-influenced style that came to be called
"death 'n' roll". Nicke Andersson later left to form the garage rock band The Hellacopters.
Legal disputes in the 2010s split the band: Petrov carried on as Entombed A.D., and he died in 2021.
