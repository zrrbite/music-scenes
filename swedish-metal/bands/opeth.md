# Opeth

- **Base:** Stockholm
- **Formed:** 1990
- **Key member:** Mikael Åkerfeldt (vocals/guitar, main songwriter)

## History
Åkerfeldt joined Opeth as a teenager and soon took over the band. From the start, *Orchid*
(1995) and *Morningrise* (1996) had long songs that moved between death metal and acoustic
folk passages, which was unusual at the time. *My Arms, Your Hearse* (1998) and *Still Life*
(1999) sharpened that approach.

*Blackwater Park* (2001), produced by Steven Wilson of Porcupine Tree, made them a major
international name. They then released the heavy *Deliverance* (2002) and the entirely clean,
mellow *Damnation* (2003) as a pair. *Ghost Reveries* (2005) and *Watershed* (2008) followed.

With *Heritage* (2011), Åkerfeldt dropped death growls completely for 70s-style progressive
rock, and the band stayed there for several albums. *The Last Will and Testament* (2024) brought
the growls back.
