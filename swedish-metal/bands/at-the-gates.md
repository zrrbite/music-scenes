# At the Gates

- **Base:** Gothenburg
- **Formed:** 1990
- **Key members:** Tomas "Tompa" Lindberg (vocals), Anders Björler (guitar), Jonas Björler (bass), Adrian Erlandsson (drums), Martin Larsson (guitar)

## History
At the Gates started out complex and experimental (*The Red in the Sky Is Ours*, 1992) and
gradually tightened their sound. *Slaughter of the Soul* (1995) is fast and concise, with
melodic but vicious riffs. It became one of the most influential metal albums of the 90s and
a direct template for 2000s American metalcore (Killswitch Engage, As I Lay Dying and many more).

The band split in 1996, right after that breakthrough. The Björler twins formed The Haunted.
At the Gates reunited in 2007 and went on to new albums, starting with *At War with Reality*
(2014). Tomas Lindberg died in 2025 **(verify)**.
