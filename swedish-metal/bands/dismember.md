# Dismember

- **Base:** Stockholm
- **Formed:** 1988. Split 2011, reunited 2019.
- **Key members:** Matti Kärki (vocals), David Blomqvist (guitar), Fred Estby (drums)

## History
Dismember was one of the core bands of the Stockholm scene, alongside Entombed and Grave.
*Like an Ever Flowing Stream* (1991), recorded at Sunlight Studio, is a pillar of the HM-2
sound with a bit more melody than its peers. It also caused a stir in the UK, where copies
were seized and the band was taken to court on obscenity charges over the lyrics of "Skin Her
Alive". They were acquitted.

*Massive Killing Capacity* (1995) and later albums kept the band at the heart of
old-school Swedish death metal until they split in 2011. They reunited for live shows in 2019.
