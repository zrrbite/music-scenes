# In Flames

- **Base:** Gothenburg
- **Formed:** 1990
- **Key members:** Jesper Strömblad (founder, guitar), Björn Gelotte (guitar, earlier drums), Anders Fridén (vocals, from 1995)

## History
Jesper Strömblad started In Flames wanting to combine death metal with Iron Maiden-style
harmonies and Swedish folk melodies. After *Lunar Strain* (1994), Anders Fridén joined on
vocals for *The Jester Race* (1996), a defining melodic death metal album. *Whoracle* (1997),
*Colony* (1999) and *Clayman* (2000) made them the scene's most popular band.

From *Reroute to Remain* (2002) on, they moved toward a more accessible, alternative-metal
sound with clean choruses. That divided longtime fans but grew their audience a lot.
Strömblad left in 2010. Later albums partly returned to heavier melodic death metal.
