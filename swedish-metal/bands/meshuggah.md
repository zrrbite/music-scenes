# Meshuggah

- **Base:** Umeå
- **Formed:** 1987
- **Members:** Jens Kidman (vocals), Fredrik Thordendal (lead guitar), Mårten Hagström (guitar), Tomas Haake (drums, lyrics), Dick Lövgren (bass)

## History
Meshuggah began as a thrash band in northern Sweden (*Contradictions Collapse*, 1991). With
*Destroy Erase Improve* (1995) they found their own sound: low, palm-muted riffs in long,
odd-numbered rhythmic cycles that clash with and resolve against the drummer's steady 4/4,
plus Thordendal's fusion-style, Allan Holdsworth-influenced leads.

*Chaosphere* (1998), *Nothing* (2002) and the single 21-minute track *Catch Thirtythree* (2005)
pushed it further, and the band moved to 8-string guitars. *obZen* (2008) is often the
entry point. *Koloss* (2012), *The Violent Sleep of Reason* (2016) and *Immutable* (2022)
followed. Their sound started the "djent" movement (Periphery, TesseracT, Animals as Leaders)
and later the Swedish thall scene.
