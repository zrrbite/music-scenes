# Amon Amarth

- **Base:** Tumba, near Stockholm
- **Formed:** 1992
- **Key members:** Johan Hegg (vocals), Olavi Mikkonen (guitar), Johan Söderberg (guitar), Ted Lundström (bass)

## History
Amon Amarth play melodic death metal about Vikings and Norse mythology: mid-tempo, anthemic
riffs, harmonized leads, and Johan Hegg's deep, clear growl. Their name comes from Tolkien (Mount
Doom). *Once Sent from the Golden Hall* (1998) and *Versus the World* (2002) built the style. *With
Oden on Our Side* (2006) and *Twilight of the Thunder God* (2008) made them headliners.

Their shows are famous for longships, giant serpents and stage battles. *Jomsviking* (2016),
*Berserker* (2019) and *The Great Heathen Army* (2022) kept them among the most popular bands in
metal.
