# Vildhjarta

- **Base:** Hudiksvall
- **Formed:** 2005
- **Key members:** Daniel Bergström and Calle Thomér (guitars), Buster Odeholm **(verify role)**; vocalist Vilhelm Bladin
- **Style:** "Thall": dissonant, atmospheric, heavily syncopated extended-range metal

## History
Vildhjarta came out of the late-2000s Meshuggah-inspired scene and quickly stood out. Instead
of clean, mathematical djent, they made something eerie and unsettling: dissonant chords,
sudden drops, odd ambient textures and huge low-end breakdowns. The band's own word for it,
**"thall"**, became the name of a subgenre.

They signed to Century Media for *Måsstaden* (2011), a concept album set around a mysterious
town **(verify)**, which became a cult classic. A long gap followed, with lineup changes and
the *Thousands of Evils* EP (2013). The second album, *Måsstaden under vatten* (2021), was
received as a worthy sequel.
