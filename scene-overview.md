# Scene Overview

Quebec, and Montreal in particular, punches well above its weight in extreme metal. People
usually put it down to a small, tight scene where musicians play in several bands at once,
a strong music-school pipeline (many players are conservatory or jazz trained), and a
francophone identity that keeps it a little separate from the rest of North America.

## Roots: the 80s

- **[Voivod](bands/voivod.md)** (Jonquière, Saguenay, formed 1982). Not death metal, but their
  dissonant chords, sci-fi concepts and odd structures shaped everything that came after.
  Gorguts' later dissonance owes them a clear debt.
- **[Obliveon](bands/obliveon.md)** and **[Necronomicon](bands/necronomicon.md)** came out of the
  late-80s thrash/death underground.

## The 90s: brutal and technical

- **[Gorguts](bands/gorguts.md)** start as a straight death metal band (*Considered Dead*, 1991)
  and arrive at *Obscura* (1998), one of the most influential avant-garde death metal records ever made.
- **[Cryptopsy](bands/cryptopsy.md)** set the template for technical brutal death metal with
  *Blasphemy Made Flesh* (1994) and *None So Vile* (1996), with Flo Mounier's drumming
  at the center of it.
- **[Kataklysm](bands/kataklysm.md)** coin the "northern hyperblast" tag.
- **[Martyr](bands/martyr.md)** (Trois-Rivières) push a more progressive, fusion-tinged approach.

## The 2000s: melodic, progressive, experimental

- **[Quo Vadis](bands/quo-vadis.md)**, **[Neuraxis](bands/neuraxis.md)** and
  **[Augury](bands/augury.md)** bring melody and progressive structure to technical death metal.
- **[Unexpect](bands/unexpect.md)** take it toward the theatrical avant-garde.
- **[Despised Icon](bands/despised-icon.md)** and **[Beneath the Massacre](bands/beneath-the-massacre.md)**
  help define deathcore and its hyper-technical edge.

## 2010s to now: the tech-prog generation

- **[Beyond Creation](bands/beyond-creation.md)** make fretless bass a scene trademark.
- **[First Fragment](bands/first-fragment.md)** go all-in on neoclassical maximalism.
- **[Chthe'ilist](bands/chtheilist.md)** revive the murky Finnish (Demilich) style.
- **[Aeternam](bands/aeternam.md)** (Quebec City) and **[Sutrah](bands/sutrah.md)** add symphonic,
  oriental and spiritual atmospheres.
- Gorguts return with *Colored Sands* (2013), with Kevin Hufnagel, Colin Marston and
  John Longstreth in the lineup.
