# Music Scenes

Notes on music scenes: where they came from, who the key bands are, how the bands connect,
and where to start listening.

## Cross-scene links
- **Tech-death:** East Coast (Death's progressive turn) → Quebec ↔ West Coast (Beyond Creation, Archspire, Zenith Passage and Revocation share a touring circuit and labels)
- **Brutality:** East Coast (Suffocation, Dying Fetus) → Brutal death → Quebec deathcore (Despised Icon, Beneath the Massacre)
- **Sweden:** Swedish metal ↔ Doom (Candlemass, Witchcraft, Graveyard) ↔ Post-metal (Cult of Luna, from Meshuggah's hometown Umeå)
- **1994:** Seattle grunge ↔ California punk (Nirvana opens the door for *Dookie* and *Smash*; Pat Smear goes Germs → Nirvana → Foo Fighters; SST releases both Black Flag and Soundgarden)
- **Djent & thall:** Swedish metal (Meshuggah, Vildhjarta, Humanity's Last Breath) → Djent & thall (Periphery, Sleep Token, Mirar), linked by Buster Odeholm; Spiritbox ↔ West Coast
- **Desert & stoner:** Doom (Kyuss) → Seattle grunge (Queens of the Stone Age, with Mark Lanegan and Dave Grohl) ↔ California punk (The Distillers' Brody Dalle)
- **Irish roots:** Dark folk (Lankum, The Pogues, Hozier) ↔ California punk (Joe Strummer, Rancid; Tom Waits on Epitaph's Anti-)
- **Heavy and slow:** Seattle grunge (Melvins) → Doom (sludge) → Post-metal (Neurosis) ↔ Dark folk (Wovenhand's heavy turn, Lankum's drones)

## Conventions
- Each scene has a `README.md` (history + band index), a scene overview (timeline), a listening
  guide, a playlist, connections (with a Mermaid family tree), sources, and a `bands/` folder.
- **(verify)** marks a fact that hasn't been checked against a reliable source yet.
