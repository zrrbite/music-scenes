# Music Scenes

Notes on music scenes: where they came from, who the key bands are, how the bands connect,
and where to start listening.

| Scene | Era | What it covers |
|-------|-----|----------------|
| [Quebec death metal](quebec/README.md) | 1988–now | Gorguts, Cryptopsy, and the technical / progressive Montreal scene (Beyond Creation, First Fragment…) |
| [US East Coast death metal](east-coast/README.md) | 1983–now | The Florida first wave (Death, Morbid Angel, Obituary), New York brutality (Suffocation, Immolation), and Boston's Revocation |
| [West Coast death metal](west-coast/README.md) | 1983–now | Bay Area origins (Possessed, Autopsy) to modern tech-death in California and Vancouver (The Faceless, Archspire, Zenith Passage…) |
| [Brutal death, slam & deathcore](brutal-death/README.md) | 1988–now | By style, not city: Suffocation → Devourment → Aborted, Despised Icon, Infant Annihilator, Organectomy |
| [Swedish metal](swedish-metal/README.md) | 1987–now | Stockholm death metal, Gothenburg melodeath, Opeth, and Meshuggah's descendants (Vildhjarta, Humanity's Last Breath…) |
| [Post-metal](post-metal/README.md) | 1985–now | Neurosis, Isis, Cult of Luna, The Ocean, Amenra, Russian Circles |
| [Doom & retro heavy rock](doom/README.md) | 1968–now | Black Sabbath, Pentagram, Candlemass, Sleep, Electric Wizard, and the Swedish retro wave (Witchcraft, Graveyard) |
| [Seattle grunge](seattle-grunge/README.md) | 1984–now | The Green River family tree: Mudhoney, Mother Love Bone, Pearl Jam, Soundgarden, Alice in Chains, Nirvana… |

## Cross-scene links
- **Tech-death:** East Coast (Death's progressive turn) → Quebec ↔ West Coast (Beyond Creation, Archspire, Zenith Passage and Revocation share a touring circuit and labels)
- **Brutality:** East Coast (Suffocation, Dying Fetus) → Brutal death → Quebec deathcore (Despised Icon, Beneath the Massacre)
- **Sweden:** Swedish metal ↔ Doom (Candlemass, Witchcraft, Graveyard) ↔ Post-metal (Cult of Luna, from Meshuggah's hometown Umeå)
- **Heavy and slow:** Seattle grunge (Melvins) → Doom (sludge) → Post-metal (Neurosis) ↔ Dark folk (Wovenhand's heavy turn, Lankum's drones)

## Conventions
- Each scene has a `README.md` (history + band index), a scene overview (timeline), a listening
  guide, a playlist, connections (with a Mermaid family tree), sources, and a `bands/` folder.
- **(verify)** marks a fact that hasn't been checked against a reliable source yet.
