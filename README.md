# Music Scenes

Notes on regional scenes: where they came from, who the key bands are, how the bands connect,
and where to start listening.

| Scene | Era | What it covers |
|-------|-----|----------------|
| [Quebec death metal](quebec/README.md) | 1988–now | Gorguts, Cryptopsy, and the technical / progressive Montreal scene (Beyond Creation, First Fragment…) |
| [Seattle grunge](seattle-grunge/README.md) | 1984–now | The Green River family tree: Mudhoney, Mother Love Bone, Pearl Jam, Soundgarden, Alice in Chains, Nirvana… |
| [Swedish metal](swedish-metal/README.md) | 1987–now | Stockholm death metal, Gothenburg melodeath, Opeth, and Meshuggah's descendants (Vildhjarta, Humanity's Last Breath…) |
| [West Coast death metal](west-coast/README.md) | 1983–now | Bay Area origins (Possessed, Autopsy) to modern tech-death in California and Vancouver (The Faceless, Archspire, Zenith Passage…) |
| [Doom & retro heavy rock](doom/README.md) | 1968–now | Black Sabbath, Pentagram, Candlemass, Sleep, Electric Wizard, and the Swedish retro wave (Witchcraft, Graveyard) |
| [Gothic Americana](gothic-americana/README.md) | 1979–now | David Eugene Edwards (16 Horsepower, Wovenhand), the Denver Sound, and roots like The Gun Club and Nick Cave |

## Cross-scene links
- **Tech-death:** Quebec ↔ West Coast (Beyond Creation, Archspire and Zenith Passage share a touring circuit and labels)
- **Sweden:** Swedish metal ↔ Doom (Candlemass, Witchcraft, Graveyard)
- **Heavy and slow:** Seattle grunge (Melvins) ↔ Doom (sludge) ↔ Gothic Americana (Wovenhand's heavy turn)

## Conventions
- Each scene has a `README.md` (history + band index) and a `bands/` folder with one page per band.
- **(verify)** marks a fact that hasn't been checked against a reliable source yet.
- Sources and accuracy notes live in each scene's folder where they exist.
