# Sources

## Accuracy notes
These pages were written from general knowledge and haven't yet been checked line by line
against references. The broad story (Green River's split, Andrew Wood's death, Temple of
the Dog, the 1991 explosion) is very well documented. Details like exact years for minor
releases and lineup changes are the most likely to need correction.

## Good references
- *Everybody Loves Our Town: An Oral History of Grunge* by Mark Yarm (2011): the definitive oral history
- *Hype!* (1996), documentary by Doug Pray
- *Pearl Jam Twenty* (2011), documentary by Cameron Crowe
- *Malfunkshun: The Andrew Wood Story* (2005), documentary
- Sub Pop's own history pages: https://www.subpop.com
- Wikipedia articles for each band (well maintained for this scene)
- Discogs (discogs.com) for release dates and labels
