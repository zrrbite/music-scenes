# Listening Guide

## The big five albums of 1991–1992
1. Nirvana – *Nevermind* (1991)
2. Pearl Jam – *Ten* (1991)
3. Soundgarden – *Badmotorfinger* (1991)
4. Alice in Chains – *Dirt* (1992)
5. Temple of the Dog – *Temple of the Dog* (1991)

## Follow the family tree in order
Hear how the sound developed, one band leading to the next:
1. Green River – *Rehab Doll* (1988)
2. Mudhoney – *Superfuzz Bigmuff* (1988)
3. Mother Love Bone – *Apple* (1990)
4. Temple of the Dog – *Temple of the Dog* (1991)
5. Pearl Jam – *Ten* (1991)
6. Mad Season – *Above* (1995)

## Heavier / closest to metal
- Soundgarden – *Superunknown* (1994)
- Alice in Chains – *Facelift* (1990), *Dirt* (1992)
- Melvins – *Houdini* (1993)

## Raw and punk
- Nirvana – *Bleach* (1989), *In Utero* (1993)
- Mudhoney – *Every Good Boy Deserves Fudge* (1991)

## Quiet and acoustic
- Alice in Chains – *Jar of Flies* (1994), *MTV Unplugged* (1996)
- Nirvana – *MTV Unplugged in New York* (1994)
- Screaming Trees – *Dust* (1996)
