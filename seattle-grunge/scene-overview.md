# Scene Overview: Timeline

The README tells the story. This page lays it out by year.

## Roots (1980–1985)
| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1980 | [Malfunkshun](bands/malfunkshun.md) formed on Bainbridge Island by Andrew and Kevin Wood |
| 1983 | [Melvins](bands/melvins.md) formed in Montesano |
| 1984 | [Green River](bands/green-river.md) and [Soundgarden](bands/soundgarden.md) formed in Seattle |
| 1985 | Green River's *Come on Down* EP, often cited as the first grunge record. [Screaming Trees](bands/screaming-trees.md) formed in Ellensburg |

## The Sub Pop years (1986–1990)
| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1986 | *Deep Six* compilation (C/Z Records). Matt Cameron joins Soundgarden |
| 1987 | Sub Pop releases Green River's *Dry as a Bone* and Soundgarden's *Screaming Life*. [Nirvana](bands/nirvana.md) and [Alice in Chains](bands/alice-in-chains.md) form |
| 1988 | Green River splits → [Mudhoney](bands/mudhoney.md) ("Touch Me I'm Sick") and [Mother Love Bone](bands/mother-love-bone.md) |
| 1989 | Nirvana's *Bleach*. Soundgarden signs to A&M (*Louder Than Love*) |
| 1990 | Andrew Wood dies in March. *Apple* released. [Temple of the Dog](bands/temple-of-the-dog.md) recorded. [Pearl Jam](bands/pearl-jam.md) forms. Alice in Chains' *Facelift*. Dave Grohl joins Nirvana |

## The explosion (1991–1994)
| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1991 | *Temple of the Dog* (April), *Ten* (August), *Nevermind* and *Badmotorfinger* (September) |
| 1992 | *Nevermind* hits number one in January. *Dirt*, *Sweet Oblivion*, and the film *Singles* |
| 1993 | *In Utero*, Pearl Jam's *Vs.* (record first-week sales), Melvins' *Houdini* |
| 1994 | *Superunknown*, *Jar of Flies*, *Vitalogy*. Kurt Cobain dies in April. Foo Fighters and [Mad Season](bands/mad-season.md) form |

## Aftermath (1995–now)
| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1995 | Mad Season's *Above*. [Foo Fighters](bands/foo-fighters.md)' debut, recorded by Grohl alone |
| 1997 | Foo Fighters' *The Colour and the Shape*. Taylor Hawkins joins |
| 1996 | *Hype!* documentary. Alice in Chains' *MTV Unplugged*. Screaming Trees' *Dust* |
| 1997 | Soundgarden breaks up |
| 1998 | Matt Cameron joins Pearl Jam |
| 2000 | Screaming Trees break up |
| 2002 | Layne Staley dies |
| 2006 | Alice in Chains return with William DuVall |
| 2010 | Soundgarden reunite |
| 2011 | Foo Fighters' *Wasting Light* (Butch Vig producing, Krist Novoselic guesting) |
| 2016 | Temple of the Dog reunion tour |
| 2017 | Chris Cornell dies |
| 2022 | Mark Lanegan dies. Taylor Hawkins dies |
