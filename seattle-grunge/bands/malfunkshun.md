# Malfunkshun

- **Formed:** 1980, Bainbridge Island, Washington
- **Members:** Andrew Wood ("Landrew the Love Child", vocals/bass), Kevin Wood (guitar), Regan Hagar (drums)

## History
One of the earliest bands in the scene, formed by brothers Andrew and Kevin Wood. Andrew's
glam-rock flamboyance (face paint, stage persona, showmanship) stood out in a scene that
mostly dressed down, and it was a big influence on how he later fronted [Mother Love Bone](mother-love-bone.md).
They appeared on *Deep Six* (1986) but never released an album while active. The posthumous
*Return to Olympus* (1995) collects their recordings. The documentary *Malfunkshun: The Andrew
Wood Story* (2005) tells his story.
