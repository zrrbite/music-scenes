# Mudhoney

- **Formed:** 1988, Seattle. Still active.
- **Members:** Mark Arm (vocals/guitar), Steve Turner (guitar), Matt Lukin (bass, until 1999; then Guy Maddison), Dan Peters (drums)

## History
Formed from the punk half of [Green River](green-river.md) (Arm and Turner) plus Matt Lukin
from the [Melvins](melvins.md). Their first single, "Touch Me I'm Sick" (1988), with its
blown-out fuzz guitar, became the anthem of the early Sub Pop sound. The *Superfuzz Bigmuff*
EP (1988), named after their guitar pedals, cemented it. The band was arguably more popular
in the UK than at home at first.

They signed to Reprise during the boom (*Piece of Cake*, 1992) but never chased the mainstream,
and they stayed sarcastic about the whole grunge industry. They went back to Sub Pop in the
2000s and have kept releasing albums, outlasting nearly all their peers.
