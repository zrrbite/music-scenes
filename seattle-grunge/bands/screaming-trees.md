# Screaming Trees

- **Formed:** 1985, Ellensburg, Washington. Broke up 2000.
- **Members:** Mark Lanegan (vocals), Gary Lee Conner (guitar), Van Conner (bass), Mark Pickerel then Barrett Martin (drums)

## History
Screaming Trees came from small-town eastern Washington rather than Seattle. They mixed 60s
psychedelia and garage rock with heavy guitars, and Mark Lanegan's deep, weathered baritone
sounded like nobody else. After a run of indie records on SST, they signed to Epic. *Sweet
Oblivion* (1992) and its single "Nearly Lost You" (on the *Singles* soundtrack) were their
commercial peak. *Dust* (1996) is often called their best album.

The band split in 2000. Lanegan went on to a long, acclaimed solo career and collaborations
with Queens of the Stone Age, Isobel Campbell and others. He died in 2022. Drummer Barrett
Martin also played in [Mad Season](mad-season.md).
