# Queens of the Stone Age

- **Base:** Palm Desert / Los Angeles, California
- **Formed:** 1996 (briefly as Gamma Ray)
- **Key member:** Josh Homme (vocals/guitar, the constant); notable past members include Nick Oliveri (bass), Mark Lanegan (vocals) and Dave Grohl (drums on *Songs for the Deaf*)

## Why it's in this section
Not a Seattle band, but closely tied to the family tree. Mark Lanegan of [Screaming
Trees](screaming-trees.md) was a member for years, and Dave Grohl of [Nirvana](nirvana.md) and
[Foo Fighters](foo-fighters.md) drummed on their best-known album. Homme's previous band
[Kyuss](../../doom/bands/kyuss.md) is covered in the Doom section.

## History
After Kyuss split, Josh Homme started Queens of the Stone Age to play "robot rock": hypnotic,
repetitive, groove-heavy riffs with a dry desert sound. The self-titled debut (1998) came first.
*Rated R* (2000), with Nick Oliveri and Mark Lanegan, was the breakthrough.

*Songs for the Deaf* (2002), with Grohl on drums and Lanegan singing on several tracks, including
"No One Knows" and "Go with the Flow", is their classic. *Lullabies to Paralyze* (2005), *Era Vulgaris*
(2007), *...Like Clockwork* (2013, Grohl guesting again), *Villains* (2017) and *In Times New Roman...*
(2023) followed. Homme also leads the long-running Desert Sessions collaborations, and he formed
Them Crooked Vultures (2009) with Grohl and Led Zeppelin's John Paul Jones.
