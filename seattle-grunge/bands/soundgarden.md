# Soundgarden

- **Formed:** 1984, Seattle. Broke up 1997, reunited 2010, inactive since Cornell's death in 2017.
- **Members:** Chris Cornell (vocals/guitar), Kim Thayil (guitar), Hiro Yamamoto (bass, then Jason Everman, then Ben Shepherd), Matt Cameron (drums, from 1986)

## History
Soundgarden was the first of the big Seattle bands to sign to a major label. Formed by
Cornell, Thayil and Yamamoto (Cornell originally drummed and sang), they combined Sabbath
heaviness with odd time signatures, drop tunings and Cornell's huge four-octave voice. Early
releases came out on Sub Pop (*Screaming Life*, 1987) and SST (*Ultramega OK*, 1988). They
signed to A&M for *Louder Than Love* (1989).

*Badmotorfinger* (1991) came out during the Nevermind boom and broke them through.
*Superunknown* (1994) went to number one in the US, with "Black Hole Sun" and "Spoonman", and
won Grammys. After *Down on the Upside* (1996) the band split in 1997.

Cornell made solo records and fronted Audioslave with members of Rage Against the Machine.
Cameron joined [Pearl Jam](pearl-jam.md) in 1998. Soundgarden reunited in 2010 and released
*King Animal* (2012). Cornell died in May 2017 in Detroit, after a show.
