# Pearl Jam

- **Formed:** 1990, Seattle. Still active.
- **Members:** Eddie Vedder (vocals), Stone Gossard (guitar), Mike McCready (guitar), Jeff Ament (bass); drummers Dave Krusen, Dave Abbruzzese, Jack Irons, then Matt Cameron (since 1998)

## History
After Mother Love Bone ended, Gossard and Ament started writing with McCready and passed an
instrumental demo around through drummer Jack Irons. Irons gave it to Eddie Vedder, a gas
station worker and singer in San Diego. Vedder wrote lyrics over it (the "Mamasan" trilogy,
which became "Alive", "Once" and "Footsteps"), mailed it back, flew up, and was in. The band
briefly went by Mookie Blaylock, after the basketball player, before becoming Pearl Jam.

*Ten* (1991) became one of the best-selling rock albums of the decade. *Vs.* (1993) set a
record for first-week sales. The band then deliberately stepped back from stardom: fewer
videos, a long fight with Ticketmaster over ticket prices, and stranger, more varied records
like *Vitalogy* (1994) and *No Code* (1996).

Matt Cameron, formerly of [Soundgarden](soundgarden.md), has drummed since 1998. The band became
known as one of the great live acts, releasing hundreds of official bootlegs of their shows,
and they've kept releasing albums into the 2020s.
