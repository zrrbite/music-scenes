# Stone Temple Pilots

- **Base:** San Diego, California
- **Formed:** 1989
- **Members:** Scott Weiland (vocals, d. 2015), Dean DeLeo (guitar), Robert DeLeo (bass), Eric Kretz (drums); later singers Chester Bennington (2013–2015) and Jeff Gutt (from 2017)

## Why it's in this section
The biggest band of the grunge era that wasn't from Seattle. Early critics dismissed them as
copycats of Pearl Jam and Alice in Chains, but their run of hits outlasted the label.

## History
*Core* (1992), with "Plush" and "Sex Type Thing", sold millions on the grunge wave. *Purple* (1994),
with "Interstate Love Song", and the glam- and psychedelia-tinged *Tiny Music... Songs from the
Vatican Gift Shop* (1996) showed a band with a much wider range. The DeLeo brothers drew on 70s
rock, Bowie and the Beatles.

Scott Weiland's addiction caused repeated breakups. He went on to front Velvet Revolver with
Slash, Duff McKagan and Matt Sorum of Guns N' Roses. Linkin Park's Chester Bennington sang with
STP from 2013 to 2015. Weiland died in 2015 and Bennington in 2017. The band continues with Jeff Gutt.
