# Mother Love Bone

- **Formed:** 1988, Seattle. Ended 1990 with Andrew Wood's death.
- **Members:** Andrew Wood (vocals), Stone Gossard (guitar), Jeff Ament (bass), Bruce Fairweather (guitar), Greg Gilmore (drums)

## History
Formed by the rock half of [Green River](green-river.md) (Gossard, Ament, Fairweather) with
[Malfunkshun](malfunkshun.md)'s Andrew Wood up front. Wood's glam showmanship and the band's
big 70s-style hard rock got them signed to PolyGram's Stardog label. Many people expected them
to be the first Seattle band to break big.

They released the *Shine* EP (1989). Days before their debut album *Apple* was due out,
Wood died of a heroin overdose in March 1990. *Apple* came out that July. The band was over,
but its grief led straight to [Temple of the Dog](temple-of-the-dog.md) and then to
[Pearl Jam](pearl-jam.md). "Chloe Dancer/Crown of Thorns" is the essential track.
