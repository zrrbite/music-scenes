# Green River

- **Formed:** 1984, Seattle. Broke up 1987–88. Occasional reunions since 2008.
- **Members:** Mark Arm (vocals), Steve Turner (guitar), Stone Gossard (guitar), Jeff Ament (bass), Alex Vincent (drums), Bruce Fairweather (guitar, replaced Turner)
- **Labels:** Homestead, Sub Pop

## History
Often called the first grunge band. Mark Arm and Steve Turner started it, and Stone Gossard
and Jeff Ament soon joined. Their records mixed Stooges-style punk with heavy, sludgy hard rock,
the basic recipe of grunge. *Come on Down* (1985) is usually cited as the first grunge record,
and the band appeared on the *Deep Six* compilation (1986). The *Dry as a Bone* EP (1987) was
one of Sub Pop's first releases.

The band split over direction during the making of *Rehab Doll* (1988). Arm and Turner wanted
to stay underground and punk, while Gossard and Ament wanted a major-label rock band. That
split produced [Mudhoney](mudhoney.md) on one side and [Mother Love Bone](mother-love-bone.md),
and eventually [Pearl Jam](pearl-jam.md), on the other.
