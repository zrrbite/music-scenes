# Foo Fighters

- **Formed:** 1994, Seattle (later based in Los Angeles). Still active.
- **Members:** Dave Grohl (vocals/guitar), Nate Mendel (bass), Pat Smear (guitar), Chris Shiflett (guitar, from 1999), Rami Jaffee (keyboards), Taylor Hawkins (drums, 1997–2022, d. 2022); early drummer William Goldsmith

## History
After Kurt Cobain died in April 1994, Dave Grohl, who had been quietly writing songs for years,
went into Robert Lang Studios in Seattle and recorded an album almost entirely by himself,
playing every instrument. He released it as *Foo Fighters* (1995), named after a WWII slang
term for UFOs, to avoid it being treated as a Grohl solo project.

To tour it he put together a band: bassist Nate Mendel and drummer William Goldsmith from
Seattle emo pioneers Sunny Day Real Estate, and Pat Smear, of LA punk band the Germs and
Nirvana's last touring lineup. *The Colour and the Shape* (1997), with "Everlong" and "Monkey
Wrench", made them one of the biggest rock bands of the era. Taylor Hawkins joined on drums
that year and became Grohl's closest musical partner.

*There Is Nothing Left to Lose* (1999), *One by One* (2002) and *In Your Honor* (2005) turned them
into a stadium band. *Wasting Light* (2011) was recorded on tape in Grohl's garage with Nevermind
producer Butch Vig, and Krist Novoselic guested on one track, a rare Nirvana reunion on record.
Taylor Hawkins died on tour in March 2022. The band's next album, *But Here We Are* (2023), was
largely about grief.

## Why it's here
Foo Fighters are the biggest branch of the grunge family tree to survive into the 21st
century: Nirvana's drummer, two members of Sunny Day Real Estate, and Nirvana's touring guitarist.
