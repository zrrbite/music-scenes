# Mad Season

- **Formed:** 1994, Seattle. Ended after Staley's death; one album.
- **Members:** Layne Staley (vocals), Mike McCready (guitar), Barrett Martin (drums), John Baker Saunders (bass, d. 1999)

## History
A Seattle supergroup that came out of rehab. Mike McCready of [Pearl Jam](pearl-jam.md) met
bassist John Baker Saunders in treatment in Minnesota. Back in Seattle they formed a band with
Barrett Martin of [Screaming Trees](screaming-trees.md), and McCready asked Layne Staley of
[Alice in Chains](alice-in-chains.md) to sing. He hoped a sober band might help Staley.

*Above* (1995) is moody and bluesy, quieter than Alice in Chains, with "River of Deceit" as its
best-known song and Mark Lanegan guesting on two tracks. Plans for a second album fell apart.
Saunders died in 1999 and Staley in 2002. A 2013 deluxe reissue added unfinished songs, some
with Lanegan singing.
