# Alice in Chains

- **Formed:** 1987, Seattle. Still active.
- **Members:** Layne Staley (vocals, d. 2002), Jerry Cantrell (guitar/vocals), Mike Starr (bass, until 1993, d. 2011), Mike Inez (bass, from 1993), Sean Kinney (drums), William DuVall (vocals/guitar, from 2006)

## History
The most metal of the big Seattle bands. Cantrell and Staley met in the late 80s Seattle
scene. Their signature sound was Cantrell's heavy, Sabbath-like riffs plus the eerie,
stacked two-part harmonies between Staley and Cantrell. They signed to Columbia, and
*Facelift* (1990) had a hit with "Man in the Box".

*Dirt* (1992) is their masterpiece. It's dark and heavy, and it's open about addiction, which
Staley was struggling with. The acoustic EPs *Sap* (1992) and *Jar of Flies* (1994) showed a
gentler side. *Jar of Flies* was the first EP ever to debut at number one in the US. Staley's
health limited the band to a final self-titled album (1995) and *MTV Unplugged* (1996). He
died in 2002.

Cantrell, Inez and Kinney came back with William DuVall in 2006. *Black Gives Way to Blue*
(2009) was a respectful return, and they've kept recording since.
