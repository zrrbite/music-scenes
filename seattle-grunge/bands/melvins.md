# Melvins

- **Formed:** 1983, Montesano, Washington
- **Key members:** Buzz Osborne ("King Buzzo", guitar/vocals), Dale Crover (drums, since 1984)
- **Style:** Sludge, slowed-down punk, experimental heavy rock

## History
The Melvins came from the logging towns of Grays Harbor, near Aberdeen. They started as a fast
hardcore band and then slowed down to a crawl, playing punk at Black Sabbath tempo. That
heaviness was a huge influence on the whole scene. A young Kurt Cobain was a fan and roadie in
Aberdeen, Dale Crover drummed on Nirvana's first demo, and bassist Matt Lukin left in 1987 to
join [Mudhoney](mudhoney.md).

The band moved to San Francisco in the late 80s and never followed a commercial path. They
signed to Atlantic during the grunge boom, released *Houdini* (1993, partly produced by
Cobain) and *Stoner Witch*, and have since released a huge, very varied catalogue. They're
still active, and they're an ancestor of sludge, stoner and drone metal as well as grunge.
