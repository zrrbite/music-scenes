# Nirvana

- **Formed:** 1987, Aberdeen, Washington. Ended 1994.
- **Members:** Kurt Cobain (vocals/guitar), Krist Novoselic (bass), Dave Grohl (drums, from 1990); earlier drummers included Aaron Burckhard, Dale Crover, Dave Foster and Chad Channing

## History
Cobain and Novoselic met in Aberdeen, a struggling logging town, through the
[Melvins](melvins.md) circle. Their first demo was recorded with Melvins drummer Dale Crover
at Jack Endino's studio. They signed to Sub Pop, and *Bleach* (1989), recorded for about $600,
was heavy and raw, though Cobain's gift for melody was already there.

Dave Grohl, from the DC hardcore band Scream, joined in 1990. The band moved to DGC (Geffen),
and *Nevermind* (1991), produced by Butch Vig, took grunge from regional scene to global
phenomenon on the back of "Smells Like Teen Spirit". Cobain was uneasy with the fame. *In Utero*
(1993), recorded with Steve Albini, was a deliberately abrasive answer to it. The *MTV Unplugged
in New York* set (recorded 1993) showed a fragile, acoustic side.

Cobain died in April 1994, which ended the band. Grohl formed Foo Fighters, and Novoselic turned
to politics and other projects.
