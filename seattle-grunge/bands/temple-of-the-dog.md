# Temple of the Dog

- **Formed:** 1990, Seattle. One album, plus a reunion tour in 2016.
- **Members:** Chris Cornell (vocals), Stone Gossard (guitar), Mike McCready (guitar), Jeff Ament (bass), Matt Cameron (drums); Eddie Vedder (guest vocals)

## History
A tribute project for Andrew Wood of [Mother Love Bone](mother-love-bone.md), who died in March
1990. Chris Cornell, Wood's roommate, had written two songs for him, "Say Hello 2 Heaven" and
"Reach Down", and recorded them with Wood's bandmates Gossard and Ament, plus McCready and
Soundgarden's Matt Cameron. The sessions grew into a full album.

Eddie Vedder had just come up from San Diego to try out for Gossard and Ament's new band. He
sang backing vocals and traded verses with Cornell on "Hunger Strike". The album came out in
April 1991 and barely sold at first. Once [Pearl Jam](pearl-jam.md) and
[Soundgarden](soundgarden.md) broke big in 1992, it was promoted again and became a hit.

The album is the clearest snapshot of the family tree: the full lineup of what would become
Pearl Jam, with members of Soundgarden, mourning the singer of Mother Love Bone.
