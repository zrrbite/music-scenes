# Necronomicon

- **Base:** La Baie (Saguenay) / Montreal
- **Formed:** 1988
- **Label:** Season of Mist
- **Style:** Death metal (often described as blackened) with occult, magic and mythology themes

## History
Necronomicon formed in 1988, rooted in La Baie in the Saguenay region and later linked to
Montreal. Founding guitarist and vocalist Rich Tanguay has led the band throughout **(verify)**.
They spent the late 80s and early 90s in the tape-trading underground (the *Morbid Ritual* demo,
1991), released the EP *The Silver Key* in 1996, and debuted with *Pharaoh of Gods* in 1999.

Releases have come slowly but steadily since then. *The Sacred Medicines* (2003) is the most
highly rated. Later albums (*Rise of the Elder Ones*, *Advent of the Human God*, *Unus*) came
out on bigger labels, with Season of Mist as the current one. They are one of the
longest-running death metal bands in Quebec.

## Sound
Death metal with black metal atmosphere and occult themes. The name comes from the fictional
book in H. P. Lovecraft's stories.

Not to be confused with the German thrash band of the same name.
