# Sutrah

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Style:** Progressive / technical death metal

## History
Sutrah is a younger Montreal band in the tech-prog lineage of Beyond Creation and Augury.
*Dunes* is their main calling card. Details about their formation and later releases still
need researching **(verify)**.

## Sound
Spiritual and atmospheric, with acoustic passages and layered fretless bass. Sits on the
"cosmic" side of the scene next to Beyond Creation and Augury.
