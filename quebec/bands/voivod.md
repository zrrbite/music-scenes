# Voivod

- **Base:** Jonquière (Saguenay)
- **Formed:** 1982
- **Key members:** Denis "Snake" Bélanger (vocals), Michel "Away" Langevin (drums, artwork), Denis "Piggy" D'Amour (guitar, d. 2005), Daniel "Chewy" Mongrain (guitar, since 2008)
- **Style:** Progressive / sci-fi thrash metal. **Not death metal**, but the scene's main ancestor.

## History
Voivod formed in Jonquière in 1982: Snake (vocals), Away (drums), Piggy (guitar) and Blacky
(bass). Their early records *War and Pain* (1984) and *Rrröööaaarrr* (1986) were raw,
punk-influenced speed metal. With *Killing Technology* (1987) and *Dimension Hatröss* (1988),
Piggy's dissonant, jazz- and prog-influenced chord voicings and the band's sci-fi concepts
built around the "Voivod" character set them apart from everything else in thrash.
*Nothingface* (1989) brought them to a major label and MTV.

Through the 90s the lineup changed: Blacky left, Snake left in the mid-90s, and Eric Forrest
sang on *Negatron* and *Phobos*. Snake returned in the early 2000s and Jason Newsted
(ex-Metallica) joined on bass. Piggy died of cancer in 2005. The band finished *Katorz*
(2006) and *Infini* (2009) using riffs he had recorded.

Daniel "Chewy" Mongrain (of [Martyr](martyr.md)) joined on guitar in 2008, starting a creative
revival: *Target Earth* (2013), *The Wake* (2018, which won a Juno Award) and *Synchro Anarchy* (2022).

## Why it's here
Piggy's dissonant chords and the band's cyberpunk concepts and odd structures fed directly into
Quebec's technical and avant-garde death metal, especially Gorguts.
