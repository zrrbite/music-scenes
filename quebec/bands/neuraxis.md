# Neuraxis

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Formed:** 1994
- **Style:** Technical death metal, growing more melodic / progressive over time

## History
Neuraxis formed in Montreal in 1994 and spent the late 90s working the local scene and
releasing early albums. *Truth Beyond...* (2001) got them noticed internationally. Each
record after that added melody and atmosphere without dropping the technical base:
*Trilateral Progression* (2005) is the one most often recommended, and *The Thin Line
Between* (2008) and *Asylon* (2011) continued the trend. Guitarist Rob Milley has been the
core member through lineup changes **(verify)**.

## Sound
Started as a fairly straight technical band and added more melody and atmosphere with each
record. *Trilateral Progression* is the usual entry point.
