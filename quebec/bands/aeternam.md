# Aeternam

- **Base:** Quebec City
- **Formed:** 2007 **(verify)**
- **Style:** Symphonic progressive death metal with Middle Eastern / North African melodies

## History
Aeternam came out of Quebec City in the late 2000s, led by vocalist and guitarist Achraf
Loudiy **(verify)**, whose North African background shapes the band's sound. *Disciples of
the Unseen* (2010) set out their style of orchestral death metal built on Middle Eastern
scales. *Moongates Guardian* (2014) got international label support **(verify: Metal Blade)**
and wider attention, and *Ruins of Empires* (2017) leaned further into epic, historical
storytelling.

## Sound
Cinematic, orchestral death metal built on oriental scales and folk melodies, with epic
historical and mythological themes. Closer to Nile or Orphaned Land in feel than to Montreal tech-death.
