# Quo Vadis

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Formed:** 1993
- **Style:** Melodic / technical death metal with progressive structures

## History
Quo Vadis formed in Montreal in 1993. *Forever...* (1996) and *Day into Night* (2000)
combined melodic death metal with classical touches, including violin, which was unusual in
the genre at the time. *Defiant Imagination* (2004) is their best-known record: tighter,
faster and more technical, with standout drumming. After that came live releases and long
quiet periods **(verify later activity)**. Their mix of melody and technicality had a big
influence on the next generation of Montreal bands.

## Sound
Soaring melodies over very technical drumming, with classical touches. Violin appears on the
earlier material. Often seen as the bridge between the 90s scene and the modern tech-prog bands.
