# Kataklysm

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Formed:** 1991
- **Key member:** Maurizio Iacono (vocals)
- **Style:** Death metal; the band calls its sound "northern hyperblast"

## History
Kataklysm formed in Montreal in 1991 with vocalist Sylvain Houde, bassist Maurizio Iacono and
guitarist Jean-François Dagenais. They signed to Nuclear Blast early. *Sorcery* (1995) and
*Temple of Knowledge* (1996) were chaotic, ultra-fast records, and the band called their style
"northern hyperblast".

Houde left after *Temple of Knowledge* and Iacono moved to vocals. From *Shadows & Dust*
(2002) on, the band streamlined its sound into groovier, more melodic death metal and toured
Europe constantly. That made them one of the most commercially successful bands from Quebec.
Iacono later started Ex Deo, a side project themed around ancient Rome.

## Sound
Early records are raw and very fast. From the 2000s on they move toward a groovier, more
melodic sound, which made them one of Quebec's most commercially successful death metal exports.
