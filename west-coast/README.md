# West Coast Death Metal (US & Canada)

From the Bay Area, where death metal got its name, to the modern technical bands of
California and Vancouver. Archspire is Canadian, so this covers the whole Pacific coast of
North America.

## History

### The Bay Area: thrash and the birth of death metal (1980s)
In the early 80s the San Francisco Bay Area was the world capital of thrash: Metallica moved
there from Los Angeles in 1983, and Exodus, Testament and Death Angel came out of the same
clubs. **[Possessed](bands/possessed.md)** pushed that sound further than anyone. Their
1985 debut *Seven Churches* ends with a track called "Death Metal", which gave the genre its
name. Chuck Schuldiner of Death briefly moved to San Francisco in the mid-80s too.

In Los Angeles, Dark Angel played some of the fastest, most technical thrash of the era with
drummer Gene Hoglan, who became one of extreme metal's most important drummers (Death,
Strapping Young Lad, Testament). In Oakland, former Death drummer Chris Reifert started
**[Autopsy](bands/autopsy.md)**, whose filthy, doom-heavy death metal is still copied today.

### Vancouver: extremity and industrial (1990s)
Vancouver had a separate, very extreme scene. Blasphemy (from nearby Burnaby) helped invent
"war metal", and Devin Townsend's **[Strapping Young Lad](bands/strapping-young-lad.md)** made
chaotic, industrial-tinged extreme metal with Gene Hoglan on drums.

### The California tech-death wave (2000s)
In the 2000s, California became a center of the new technical death metal. Santa Cruz's
**[Decrepit Birth](bands/decrepit-birth.md)** went from brutal to cosmic and Death-inspired.
Los Angeles' **[The Faceless](bands/the-faceless.md)**, led by Michael Keene, mixed technical
death metal, progressive structures and vocoder and clean vocals. *Planetary Duality* (2008) is a
landmark of the era. Labels like Sumerian Records and Unique Leader (also Californian) put out
much of this music.

### The modern generation (2010s – now)
**[Fallujah](bands/fallujah.md)** added ambient textures and soaring leads. **[Rings of
Saturn](bands/rings-of-saturn.md)** pushed speed and alien sci-fi themes to absurd extremes.
**[Zenith Passage](bands/zenith-passage.md)**, founded by guitarist Justin McKinney, brought a
dense, futuristic sound. In Vancouver, **[Archspire](bands/archspire.md)** became famous for
almost unbelievable speed and rapid-fire vocals. They are a close cousin of the Quebec
tech-death scene: same era, same tours, similar aesthetic.

If you like [Quebec's Beyond Creation and First Fragment](../quebec/README.md), this is the closest neighboring scene.
