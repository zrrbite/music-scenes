# Possessed

- **Base:** San Francisco Bay Area
- **Formed:** 1983. Split 1987. Re-formed 2007.
- **Key members:** Jeff Becerra (vocals/bass), Mike Torrao (guitar), Larry LaLonde (guitar), Mike Sus (drums)

## History
Possessed were Bay Area teenagers who took thrash further than anyone around them: faster,
nastier, with growled vocals and satanic lyrics. *Seven Churches* (1985) is often called the
first death metal album, and its closing track "Death Metal" is generally credited with naming
the genre. *Beyond the Gates* (1986) and the *Eyes of Horror* EP (1987) followed before the
band split.

Guitarist Larry LaLonde later joined Primus. In 1989 Jeff Becerra was shot in a robbery and
left paralyzed from the chest down. He re-formed Possessed in 2007 with new musicians and
released *Revelations of Oblivion* in 2019, 33 years after the band's previous album.
