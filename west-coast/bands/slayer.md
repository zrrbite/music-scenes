# Slayer

- **Base:** Huntington Park, Los Angeles
- **Formed:** 1981. Farewell tour ended 2019; occasional reunion shows from 2024.
- **Members:** Tom Araya (bass/vocals), Kerry King (guitar), Jeff Hanneman (guitar, d. 2013), Dave Lombardo (drums; later Paul Bostaph), Gary Holt (guitar, from 2011; of Exodus)

## Why it's in this section
Slayer were thrash, but they were the most extreme band of the 80s, and their speed, satanic
themes and chaotic solos are a direct bridge to death metal. Bands like Possessed came straight
out of their wake.

## History
After *Show No Mercy* (1983, Metal Blade) and *Hell Awaits* (1985), Slayer signed to Rick Rubin's
Def Jam. *Reign in Blood* (1986) is 29 minutes of relentless speed, with "Angel of Death" and "Raining
Blood", and it's often called the most extreme metal album of its era. *South of Heaven* (1988)
deliberately slowed down, and *Seasons in the Abyss* (1990) balanced the two approaches. Lombardo's
double-bass drumming was hugely influential.

Slayer is one of the thrash "Big Four" with Metallica, Megadeth and Anthrax. Jeff Hanneman died
in 2013 and was replaced by Gary Holt of Bay Area thrash band Exodus. The farewell tour ended in 2019.
