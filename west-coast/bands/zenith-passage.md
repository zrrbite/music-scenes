# Zenith Passage

- **Base:** Los Angeles area, California **(verify)**
- **Formed:** 2011
- **Key member:** Justin McKinney (guitar, founder)
- **Style:** Technical / progressive death metal

## History
Guitarist Justin McKinney founded Zenith Passage. He's also associated with [The
Faceless](the-faceless.md) **(verify)**. The *Cosmic Dissonance* EP (2013) introduced their
dense, futuristic, very technical sound, and the debut *Solipsist* (2016) on Unique Leader
earned wide praise in tech-death circles for its complexity and sci-fi atmosphere.

A long gap with lineup changes followed before *Datalysium* (2023) **(verify)**, which came
with a new vocalist and a sharper concept about technology and data.
