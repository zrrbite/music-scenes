# Fallujah

- **Base:** San Francisco Bay Area
- **Formed:** 2007
- **Key member:** Scott Carstairs (guitar)

## History
Fallujah began as a deathcore band and grew into one of the most atmospheric technical death
metal acts around. Carstairs' soaring, reverb-drenched leads and ambient, electronic textures
gave them a dreamy feel unusual in the genre. After *The Harvest Wombs* (2011), *The Flesh
Prevails* (2014) on Nuclear Blast was their breakthrough. *Dreamless* (2016) added more clean
female vocals and electronics. After lineup changes they released *Undying Light* (2019)
and *Empyrean* (2022).
