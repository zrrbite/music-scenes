# Decrepit Birth

- **Base:** Santa Cruz, California
- **Formed:** 2001
- **Key members:** Bill Robinson (vocals), Matt Sotelo (guitar)

## History
Decrepit Birth started out playing straight brutal death metal (*...And Time Begins*, 2003).
Over time they took on the melodic, progressive style of late-era Death, with cosmic and
spiritual lyrics. *Diminishing Between Worlds* (2008) and especially *Polarity* (2010) made
them a key band of the late-2000s tech-death wave. *Axis Mundi* (2017) followed after a long gap.
Their Dan Seagrave cover art is part of their identity.
