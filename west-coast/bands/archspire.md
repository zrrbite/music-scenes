# Archspire

- **Base:** Vancouver, British Columbia
- **Formed:** 2009
- **Members:** Oliver Rae Aleron (vocals), Dean Lamb (guitar), Tobi Morelli (guitar), Jared Smith (bass), Spencer Prewett (drums)
- **Style:** Hyper-technical death metal

## History
Archspire are known as one of the fastest, most precise bands in metal. Oliver Rae Aleron's
vocals are delivered like rapid-fire rap ("shotgun vocals"), and the songs move at
extreme tempos with almost classical counterpoint between the guitars.

After *All Shall Align* (2011) and *The Lucid Collective* (2014), they signed to Season of Mist,
the same label as Beyond Creation. *Relentless Mutation* (2017) made them famous worldwide, and
*Bleed the Future* (2021) is considered their peak. They're part of the same tech-death touring
circuit as the Quebec bands.
