# The Faceless

- **Base:** Encino, Los Angeles
- **Formed:** 2004
- **Key member:** Michael Keene (guitar, clean vocals, producer); many lineup changes

## History
Michael Keene founded The Faceless as a teenager. After the brutal-leaning debut *Akeldama*
(2006), *Planetary Duality* (2008), released on Sumerian Records, combined technical death
metal with progressive structures, vocoder vocals and sci-fi themes about ancient aliens. It
became one of the defining tech-death albums of its decade.

*Autotheism* (2012) was more progressive and theatrical, with more clean vocals. The band had
constant lineup turnover, and by *In Becoming a Ghost* (2017) Keene was nearly the only original
member left. Activity since has been sporadic.
