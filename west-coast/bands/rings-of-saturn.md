# Rings of Saturn

- **Base:** San Francisco Bay Area
- **Formed:** 2009
- **Key member:** Lucas Mann (guitar)
- **Style:** Technical deathcore with alien / sci-fi themes ("aliencore")

## History
Rings of Saturn appeared online with a sound of very fast, chaotic sweep picking, blast beats
and alien-invasion lyrics, and their fans named it "aliencore". *Dingir* (2010) and *Lugal Ki
En* (2014) put them on the map. The band has faced repeated accusations that its studio
recordings are sped up beyond what's played live, which the band has disputed **(verify
details)**. Love them or hate them, they're a symbol of the most extreme end of tech-death.
