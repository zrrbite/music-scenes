# Autopsy

- **Base:** Oakland, California
- **Formed:** 1987. Split 1995. Reunited 2008/2009.
- **Key members:** Chris Reifert (drums, vocals), Eric Cutler (guitar), Danny Coralles (guitar)

## History
Chris Reifert had drummed on Death's *Scream Bloody Gore* (1987). Back in the Bay Area he
started Autopsy, doing the opposite of technical: sludgy, gory, sickening death metal that
slowed to doom crawls between bursts of speed. *Severed Survival* (1989) and *Mental Funeral*
(1991) are classics, and *Mental Funeral* is a foundation of death/doom.

The band split in the mid-90s (Reifert went on to Abscess), reunited at the end of the 2000s,
and has been releasing records since. Their influence on today's "cavernous" and "old-school"
revival bands is huge.
