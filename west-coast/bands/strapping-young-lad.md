# Strapping Young Lad

- **Base:** Vancouver
- **Formed:** 1994. Ended 2007.
- **Members:** Devin Townsend (vocals/guitar), Gene Hoglan (drums), Jed Simon (guitar), Byron Stroud (bass)

## History
Devin Townsend had sung for Steve Vai and been put off by the music industry. Strapping
Young Lad started as an outlet for that anger. *Heavy as a Really Heavy Thing* (1995) was close
to a solo project. *City* (1997), with the full lineup and Gene Hoglan's drumming, is the
classic: a dense, industrial-tinged wall of sound that sounds like nothing else.

After a hiatus, the self-titled album (2003), *Alien* (2005) and *The New Black* (2006)
followed. Townsend ended the band in 2007 to focus on his solo work, which covers ambient,
progressive, pop and metal.
