# Deftones

- **Base:** Sacramento, California
- **Formed:** 1988
- **Members:** Chino Moreno (vocals), Stephen Carpenter (guitar), Abe Cunningham (drums), Frank Delgado (keyboards/samples), Chi Cheng (bass, until 2008, d. 2013), Sergio Vega (bass, 2009–2021)

## Why it's in this section
Deftones aren't death metal, but they're the West Coast's great atmospheric heavy band. Their
mix of crushing riffs and dreamy, shoegaze-like texture overlaps a lot with
[post-metal](../../post-metal/README.md). Chino Moreno even formed Palms with three former members of Isis.

## History
Deftones came up in the 90s nu-metal wave with *Adrenaline* (1995) and *Around the Fur* (1997), but
always stood apart. *White Pony* (2000), with "Change (In the House of Flies)" and the
Grammy-winning "Elite", brought in ambient, trip-hop and new wave influences and became a classic.
Stephen Carpenter's 7- and 8-string guitars kept the heaviness.

Bassist Chi Cheng was badly injured in a 2008 car crash and died in 2013. The band channeled that
into *Diamond Eyes* (2010) and *Koi No Yokan* (2012), two of their most loved albums. *Ohms* (2020)
and *Private Music* (2025) **(verify)** followed. Moreno's side projects include Crosses, Team Sleep
and Palms.
