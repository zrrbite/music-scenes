# Kataklysm

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Formed:** 1991
- **Key member:** Maurizio Iacono (vocals)
- **Style:** Death metal; the band calls its sound "northern hyperblast"

## Sound
Early records are raw and very fast. From the 2000s on they move toward a groovier, more
melodic sound, which made them one of Quebec's most commercially successful death metal exports.
