# Despised Icon

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Formed:** 2002
- **Style:** Deathcore / brutal death metal

## History
Despised Icon formed in Montreal in 2002 and quickly became one of the bands that defined
deathcore. After the debut *Consumed by Your Poison* (2002), Alex Erian joined Steve Marois to
make a two-vocalist lineup **(verify)**. They signed to Century Media for *The Healing Process*
(2005) and *The Ills of Modern Man* (2007), which put them at the front of the genre
internationally.

After *Day of Mourning* (2009), the band split in 2010. Several members went on to Obey the
Brave. They reunited in 2014 and have since released *Beast* (2016), *Purgatory* (2019) and
*Shadow Work* (2022).

## Sound
One of the bands that defined deathcore: brutal death metal blasting, hardcore breakdowns, and
two vocalists (Alex Erian and Steve Marois).
