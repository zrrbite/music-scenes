# Neuraxis

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Formed:** 1994
- **Style:** Technical death metal, growing more melodic / progressive over time

## Sound
Started as a fairly straight technical band and added more melody and atmosphere with each
record. *Trilateral Progression* is the usual entry point.
