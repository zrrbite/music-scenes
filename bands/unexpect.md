# Unexpect

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Formed:** 1996; split in 2015
- **Style:** Avant-garde extreme metal (progressive, death, black, circus-like theatrics)

## History
Unexpect formed in Montreal in 1996 and spent years building a cult following with
independent releases. *In a Flesh Aquarium* (2006) brought them international notice:
male and female vocals, violin, Chaoth's 9-string bass, and songs that swing between black
metal blasts, circus melodies and jazz. *Fables of the Sleepless Empire* (2011) was more
polished and orchestral. The band announced its breakup in 2015. They remain a reference
point for avant-garde metal well beyond Quebec.

## Sound
Chaotic and theatrical, with several vocalists, violin, and a 9-string bass played by Chaoth.
More avant-garde than death metal, but it grew out of the same scene.
