# Beneath the Massacre

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Formed:** 2004
- **Style:** Technical death metal / deathcore

## History
Beneath the Massacre formed in Montreal in 2004. They signed to Prosthetic Records and
released *Mechanics of Dysfunction* (2007) and *Dystopia* (2008) in quick succession, earning
a reputation for some of the fastest, most mechanical playing in the deathcore/tech-death
crossover. *Incongruous* (2012) followed. A long gap came after that, before *Fearmonger*
**(verify year)**.

## Sound
Extremely fast and mechanical: machine-gun riffing, constant blasting, very little let-up.
