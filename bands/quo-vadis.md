# Quo Vadis

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Formed:** 1993
- **Style:** Melodic / technical death metal with progressive structures

## Sound
Soaring melodies over very technical drumming, with classical touches. Violin appears on the
earlier material. Often seen as the bridge between the 90s scene and the modern tech-prog bands.
