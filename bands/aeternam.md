# Aeternam

- **Base:** Quebec City
- **Formed:** 2007 **(verify)**
- **Style:** Symphonic progressive death metal with Middle Eastern / North African melodies

## Sound
Cinematic, orchestral death metal built on oriental scales and folk melodies, with epic
historical and mythological themes. Closer to Nile or Orphaned Land in feel than to Montreal tech-death.
