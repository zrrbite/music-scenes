# Cryptopsy

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Formed:** 1988 as Necrosis, renamed Cryptopsy in 1992
- **Key member:** Flo Mounier (drums)
- **Style:** Brutal / technical death metal

## History
The band began in Montreal in 1988 as Necrosis and took the name Cryptopsy in 1992. The
debut *Blasphemy Made Flesh* (1994) introduced vocalist Lord Worm's horror lyrics and
Flo Mounier's very fast, inventive drumming. *None So Vile* (1996) refined the formula and
became a genre benchmark.

Lord Worm left in 1997. Mike DiSalvo sang on *Whisper Supremacy* (1998) and *...And Then
You'll Beg* (2000), which pushed the technical side further. Lord Worm came back for *Once
Was Not* (2005) and then left again.

With vocalist Matt McGachy, *The Unspoken King* (2008) brought in clean vocals and deathcore
elements, and longtime fans rejected it. The band went back to its roots with the
self-released *Cryptopsy* (2012) and *The Book of Suffering* EPs, and *As Gomorrah Burns*
(2023) was received as a return to form. Through every lineup change, Mounier has been the
one constant.

## Sound
Relentless speed, very technical drumming and chaotic song structures. *None So Vile* is often
named as one of the best brutal death metal albums ever made, largely thanks to Mounier's
drumming and Lord Worm's vocals.
