# Obliveon

- **Base:** Longueuil / Montreal
- **Formed:** 1987 (as Oblivion; renamed Obliveon in 1989)
- **Active:** 1989–2002, a one-off 2007 reunion, 2008, and 2014–present
- **Style:** Technical death/thrash metal early on, then groove metal, and later industrial/groove

## History
The band started in 1987 on Montreal's South Shore as Oblivion and became Obliveon in 1989.
*From This Day Forward* (1990) and *Nemesis* (1993) put them among the most respected
technical thrash/death bands in Canada, often compared with Voivod for their futuristic
themes and intricate playing.

Like many bands of the era, they changed direction in the mid-90s. *Cybervoid* (1996) and
*Carnivore Mothermouth* (1999) leaned toward groove and industrial metal. The band broke up
in 2002. They reunited for a one-off show at Montreal's Club Soda in 2007, celebrating 25
years of Quebec metal, and have been active again since 2014.

## Sound
A bridge between Voivod-style technical thrash and the death metal that came after:
fast, intricate and a little cold-sounding, with themes of futurism, technology and psychology.
The later albums move away from death/thrash.
