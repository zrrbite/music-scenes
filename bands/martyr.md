# Martyr

- **Base:** Trois-Rivières
- **Formed:** 1994
- **Key members:** Daniel Mongrain (guitar, vocals), François Mongrain (bass)
- **Style:** Technical / progressive death metal

## History
Martyr formed in Trois-Rivières in 1994 around brothers Daniel and François Mongrain. Their
debut *Hopeless Hopes* (1997) already showed a progressive bent. *Warp Zone* (2000) became
their calling card: technical death metal with fusion-like phrasing and rhythmic
playfulness, at a time when few bands were working that way.

*Feeding the Abscess* (2006) was heavier and more focused. In 2008 Daniel Mongrain joined
Voivod, taking the guitar role left by the late Piggy D'Amour, and Martyr's activity slowed a
lot after that **(verify current status)**. The band is a direct stylistic ancestor of the
Montreal tech-prog bands that followed.

## Sound
Complex, fusion-tinged technical death metal with very clean, precise guitar work. An early
example of the conservatory-trained, prog-leaning approach that later Quebec bands made famous.
