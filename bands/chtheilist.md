# Chthe'ilist

- **Base:** Montreal area. The Gemini draft said Longueuil/McMasterville **(verify)**
- **Key member:** Phil Tougas (also in [First Fragment](first-fragment.md))
- **Style:** Old-school / avant-garde death metal

## Sound
The opposite of First Fragment's bright shred: murky, cavernous and strange, very much in the
spirit of Finland's Demilich. Gurgling vocals, odd riff shapes and cosmic horror themes.
