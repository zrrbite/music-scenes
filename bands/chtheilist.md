# Chthe'ilist

- **Base:** Montreal area. The Gemini draft said Longueuil/McMasterville **(verify)**
- **Key member:** Phil Tougas (also in [First Fragment](first-fragment.md))
- **Style:** Old-school / avant-garde death metal

## History
Chthe'ilist started in the early 2010s **(verify)** as an outlet for Phil Tougas' love of
early-90s Finnish death metal, especially Demilich and Abhorrence. After demos, the full-length
*Le Dernier Crépuscule* (2016) came out on Profound Lore. It was praised as one of the best
modern takes on that strange, cavernous style, with lyrics about cosmic horror and a band
mythology all its own.

## Sound
The opposite of First Fragment's bright shred: murky, cavernous and strange, very much in the
spirit of Finland's Demilich. Gurgling vocals, odd riff shapes and cosmic horror themes.
