# Necronomicon

- **Base:** Beloeil (Montérégie, near Montreal). The Gemini draft said Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean, which looks wrong; see [sources](../sources.md).
- **Formed:** late 1980s **(verify)**
- **Style:** Blackened death metal with mystical / occult themes

## Sound
Death metal with black metal atmosphere and occult themes.

Not to be confused with the German thrash band of the same name.
