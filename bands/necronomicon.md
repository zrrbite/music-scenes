# Necronomicon

- **Base:** La Baie (Saguenay) / Montreal
- **Formed:** 1988
- **Label:** Season of Mist
- **Style:** Death metal (often described as blackened) with occult, magic and mythology themes

## Sound
Death metal with black metal atmosphere and occult themes. The name comes from the fictional
book in H. P. Lovecraft's stories.

Not to be confused with the German thrash band of the same name.
