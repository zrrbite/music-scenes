# First Fragment

- **Base:** Montreal area **(verify exact town)**
- **Formed:** 2007 **(verify)**
- **Key member:** Phil Tougas (guitar, main songwriter)
- **Style:** Neoclassical technical death metal

## History
First Fragment formed in the late 2000s **(verify)** around guitarist and songwriter Phil
Tougas. After years of demos and lineup changes, they released *Dasein* (2016) on Unique
Leader **(verify)**, a debut packed with neoclassical shred, flamenco passages and slap bass.

*Gloire Éternelle* (2021) took it further: longer songs, denser arrangements, and some of the
most extravagant bass playing in the genre. It was one of the most talked-about tech-death
records of its year. Tougas also leads the much darker [Chthe'ilist](chtheilist.md).

## Sound
Maximalism with no apologies: neoclassical sweep picking, twin-lead guitars, flamenco-style
passages and slap bass, all at full speed. Exhausting in the best way. *Gloire Éternelle* is
known for its bass work as much as its shred.
