# Voivod

- **Base:** Jonquière (Saguenay)
- **Formed:** 1982
- **Key members:** Denis "Snake" Bélanger (vocals), Michel "Away" Langevin (drums, artwork), Denis "Piggy" D'Amour (guitar, d. 2005), Daniel "Chewy" Mongrain (guitar, since 2008)
- **Style:** Progressive / sci-fi thrash metal. **Not death metal**, but the scene's main ancestor.

## Why it's here
Piggy's dissonant chords and the band's cyberpunk concepts and odd structures fed directly into
Quebec's technical and avant-garde death metal, especially Gorguts.
