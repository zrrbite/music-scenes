# Beyond Creation

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Formed:** 2005
- **Key members:** Simon Girard (guitar, vocals); bass by Dominic "Forest" Lapointe, then Hugo Doyon-Karout
- **Style:** Technical / progressive death metal

## Sound
Makes very complex material sound smooth and coherent. The fretless bass carries melodic
lines rather than just following the guitars, and has become the band's signature. *The Aura*
manages to flow despite how dense it is.
