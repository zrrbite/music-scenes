# Beyond Creation

- **Base:** Montreal
- **Formed:** 2005
- **Key members:** Simon Girard (guitar, vocals); bass by Dominic "Forest" Lapointe, then Hugo Doyon-Karout
- **Style:** Technical / progressive death metal

## History
Guitarist and vocalist Simon Girard founded Beyond Creation in Montreal in 2005. Their debut
*The Aura* came out independently in 2011 and spread fast by word of mouth; Dominic "Forest"
Lapointe's fretless bass got a lot of the attention. They signed to Season of Mist, which
reissued *The Aura* and released *Earthborn Evolution* (2014). That record made them one of
the leading tech-death bands of the decade, with heavy international touring.

Lapointe left in the mid-2010s and Hugo Doyon-Karout took over the fretless role. Both
*Algorythm* (2018) and the later *Infinite Static* **(verify)** feature him.

## Sound
Makes very complex material sound smooth and coherent. The fretless bass carries melodic
lines rather than just following the guitars, and has become the band's signature. *The Aura*
manages to flow despite how dense it is.
