# Black Sabbath

- **Base:** Birmingham, England
- **Formed:** 1968 (first as Polka Tulk and Earth). Final show 2017, plus a farewell reunion show in 2025.
- **Classic lineup:** Ozzy Osbourne (vocals), Tony Iommi (guitar), Geezer Butler (bass, lyrics), Bill Ward (drums)

## History
Four working-class kids from industrial Birmingham, inspired by blues and horror films,
created heavy metal. Tony Iommi lost the tips of two fingers in a sheet-metal factory
accident. He made prosthetic fingertips and tuned down to make playing easier, which
gave the band its low, heavy sound.

The debut *Black Sabbath* (1970), with its title track built on the tritone, and *Paranoid*
(1970) defined the genre. *Master of Reality* (1971) went even lower and slower, with "Sweet
Leaf" and "Children of the Grave". It's the most direct ancestor of doom and stoner metal.
*Vol. 4* (1972) and *Sabbath Bloody Sabbath* (1973) followed.

Ozzy was fired in 1979. Ronnie James Dio replaced him for *Heaven and Hell* (1980), and many
lineups followed. The original band reunited several times, with a final album *13* (2013)
and a farewell tour ending in Birmingham in 2017. Ozzy Osbourne died in 2025, weeks after a
final reunion show in Birmingham **(verify)**.
