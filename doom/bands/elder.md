# Elder

- **Base:** Massachusetts (Fall River / Boston area); leader Nick DiSalvo later relocated to Berlin **(verify)**
- **Formed:** 2006 **(verify)**
- **Members:** Nick DiSalvo (vocals/guitar, songwriter), Jack Donovan (bass), Matt Couto (drums, until about 2019; later Georg Edert), Michael Risberg (guitar/keys, from about 2017) **(verify lineup years)**
- **Style:** Progressive stoner / psychedelic doom

## History
Elder began as a heavy, Sleep- and Kyuss-inspired stoner doom trio. The self-titled debut
(2008) and *Dead Roots Stirring* (2011) were thick with fuzz. From there Nick DiSalvo's
songwriting grew far more ambitious: long, multi-part songs with progressive-rock structures,
intertwined melodic guitar lines, and big, uplifting climaxes alongside the heavy riffs.

*Lore* (2015) was the breakthrough and is often named one of the best stoner/psych records of its
decade. *Reflections of a Floating World* (2017) added a second guitarist and keyboards. *Omens*
(2020) and *Innate Passage* (2022) moved further toward 70s prog and krautrock. They're probably
the best-known band of the modern progressive stoner scene, a bridge between Kyuss's desert
heaviness and prog rock.
