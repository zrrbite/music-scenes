# Cathedral

- **Base:** Coventry, England
- **Formed:** 1989. Ended 2013.
- **Key members:** Lee Dorrian (vocals), Garry Jennings (guitar)

## History
Lee Dorrian sang on Napalm Death's grindcore landmark *Scum*, then left to do the opposite.
Cathedral's debut *Forest of Equilibrium* (1991) is one of the slowest, heaviest albums ever
made. Their sound then moved toward groovier, 70s-style heavy rock with *The Ethereal Mirror*
(1993) and later records. They released ten albums before ending with *The Last Spire* (2013).

Dorrian's label **Rise Above Records** may be his biggest legacy. It became the home of the
doom and retro rock underground, including Electric Wizard, Witchcraft, Pentagram reissues,
Uncle Acid and Blood Ceremony.
