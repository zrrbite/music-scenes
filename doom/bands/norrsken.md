# Norrsken

- **Base:** Örebro, Sweden
- **Active:** 1995–2000 (started under the name Winterorb) **(verify)**
- **Members:** Joakim Nilsson (vocals), Magnus Pelander (guitar), Rikard Edlund (bass), Kristoffer Sjödahl (drums) **(verify)**
- **Style:** Psychedelic / heavy rock with stoner and doom weight
- **Name:** Swedish for "aurora borealis"

## History
Norrsken never released a full-length album, but it is the common root of the two main bands
of the Swedish retro wave. They played heavy, fuzzy, 70s-inspired rock at a time when most
Swedish heavy bands were playing melodic death metal. Their recorded output was a few demos, the
7" single "Armageddon" / "Little Lady" (1999), and tracks on tribute albums to Blue Cheer and
Trouble (including a cover of Trouble's "Psalm 9") **(verify)**.

### Influences: Pentagram above all
Norrsken worshipped underground 70s heavy rock, especially Pentagram, Black Sabbath, Sir Lord
Baltimore and Captain Beyond. According to Pelander in later interviews, they were fully
immersed in the period and dressed in authentic 1970s clothing for their shows **(verify)**.
They were also part of the 90s underground tribute-album scene that helped revive interest in
Pentagram and Bobby Liebling.

So Witchcraft wasn't a sudden discovery of Pentagram. Pelander took the doom element that
Norrsken had been mixing with garage psych and isolated it in its purest form.

### The split (2000)
When the band ended in 2000 it split into separate lines:
- **Magnus Pelander** founded [Witchcraft](witchcraft.md) in 2000. It began as a one-off tribute
  to Pentagram and Roky Erickson (The 13th Floor Elevators) and grew into a full band.
- **Joakim Nilsson** and **Rikard Edlund** played together in Albatross **(verify)**, then
  co-founded [Graveyard](graveyard.md) in Gothenburg in 2006.
- **Kristoffer Sjödahl** became a founding member of the Örebro band Dead Man **(verify)**.

### Why the newer bands sound older
Norrsken's recordings are raw 90s demos and sound like a 90s stoner/garage band. Witchcraft
deliberately recorded its 2004 debut with vintage 70s gear so that it would sound like a lost
early-70s record, so it sounds *older* than its predecessor. Graveyard took the same 70s
influences (Led Zeppelin, Cream, Free) and recorded them with punchy, modern studio fidelity.

| Band | Active | Production | Sound |
|------|--------|------------|-------|
| Norrsken | 1995–2000 | Raw 90s demos | 90s stoner / psychedelic rock |
| [Witchcraft](witchcraft.md) | 2000– | Vintage 70s analog gear | 1970s occult rock and proto-doom |
| [Graveyard](graveyard.md) | 2006– | Modern, sharp studio sound | High-energy retro rock / heavy blues |
