# Type O Negative

- **Base:** Brooklyn, New York
- **Active:** 1989–2010
- **Members:** Peter Steele (vocals/bass, d. 2010), Kenny Hickey (guitar), Josh Silver (keyboards), Sal Abruscato then Johnny Kelly (drums)
- **Style:** Gothic doom metal

## History
Peter Steele had led the crossover thrash band Carnivore. With Type O Negative ("the Drab Four")
he went somewhere else: slow, Sabbath-heavy doom riffs with Beatles melodies, lush keyboards,
gothic romance, and his very deep baritone. They were also full of dark, self-mocking humor.

*Slow, Deep and Hard* (1991) was abrasive. *Bloody Kisses* (1993), with "Black No. 1" and "Christian
Woman", was a breakthrough and Roadrunner's first platinum album. *October Rust* (1996) was lush and
romantic, *World Coming Down* (1999) bleak and despairing. *Dead Again* (2007) was their last. Steele
died in 2010 and the band ended. Their mix of doom and goth influenced countless bands.
