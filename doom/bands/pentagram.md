# Pentagram

- **Base:** Virginia / Washington DC area
- **Formed:** 1971
- **Key members:** Bobby Liebling (vocals, the constant), Victor Griffin (guitar, on and off)

## History
Pentagram is one of the great "should have been" stories in rock. Bobby Liebling's band
played Sabbath-style heavy rock in the early 70s, recorded demos and singles, and came close
to deals several times, but drug problems, lineup chaos and Liebling's own behavior
kept derailing them.

A 1980s lineup with Victor Griffin finally released an album in 1985, later reissued as
*Relentless*, followed by *Day of Reckoning* (1987). Those records, plus compilations of 70s
recordings (*First Daze Here*, released by Rise Above), turned Pentagram into a cult band
and a template for traditional doom.

The documentary *Last Days Here* (2011) followed Liebling's near-fatal addiction and comeback.
Witchcraft started partly as an homage to Pentagram's sound.
