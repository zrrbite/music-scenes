# Candlemass

- **Base:** Stockholm, Sweden
- **Formed:** 1984
- **Key members:** Leif Edling (bass, songwriter), Messiah Marcolin (vocals, 1987–1991 and later), Johan Längqvist (vocals on the debut, returned 2018)

## History
Leif Edling formed Candlemass after his earlier band Nemesis. The debut *Epicus Doomicus
Metallicus* (1986), sung by session vocalist Johan Längqvist, combined Sabbath-slow riffs
with grand, classical, almost operatic drama. It named and defined "epic doom metal", and
"Solitude" is its anthem.

Messiah Marcolin, known for his monk's robe and big voice, fronted the classic run *Nightfall*
(1987), *Ancient Dreams* (1988) and *Tales of Creation* (1989). After many lineup changes and
reunions, Längqvist returned for *The Door to Doom* (2019), which features a guest solo
from Tony Iommi.
