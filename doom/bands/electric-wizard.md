# Electric Wizard

- **Base:** Dorset, England
- **Formed:** 1993
- **Key member:** Jus Oborn (vocals/guitar); Liz Buckingham (guitar, since 2003)

## History
Jus Oborn's band took Sabbath's sound and soaked it in fuzz, distortion and horror-film
imagery: occultism, 70s exploitation movies, H. P. Lovecraft and drug culture. *Come My
Fanatics...* (1997) and especially *Dopethrone* (2000) on Rise Above are landmarks of
stoner/sludge doom, a wall of filthy low end.

After the original lineup fell apart, Oborn rebuilt the band with Liz Buckingham. *We Live*
(2004), *Witchcult Today* (2007) and *Time to Die* (2014) leaned into a more vintage, occult
style. They're one of the most influential doom bands of their generation.
