# Sleep

- **Base:** San Jose, California
- **Formed:** 1990. Split late 1990s. Reunited 2009.
- **Members:** Al Cisneros (bass/vocals), Matt Pike (guitar), Chris Hakius (drums, later Jason Roeder of Neurosis)

## History
Sleep turned the Sabbath riff into a ritual. *Holy Mountain* (1992, on Earache) is a stoner
doom classic. They then signed to London Records and spent years writing one song: an
hour-long, slow-motion pilgrimage about weed and the desert. The label refused to release it
and the band split. The recording later came out in versions as *Jerusalem* (1999) and
*Dopesmoker* (2003), and it's now considered a masterpiece of the genre.

Cisneros and Hakius formed the meditative Om, and Pike formed the ferocious High on Fire.
Sleep reunited in 2009 and released *The Sciences* (2018) as a surprise on April 20.
