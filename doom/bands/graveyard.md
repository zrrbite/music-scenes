# Graveyard

- **Base:** Gothenburg (the Hisingen district), Sweden
- **Formed:** 2006
- **Key members:** Joakim Nilsson (vocals/guitar), Rikard Edlund (bass), Axel Sjöberg (drums), Jonatan LaRocca-Ramm (guitar)
- **Style:** Retro heavy blues rock with doom weight

## History
Graveyard came out of the same circle as Witchcraft. Nilsson and Edlund had played in [Norrsken](norrsken.md)
with Magnus Pelander, and after some years apart they formed Graveyard. Their sound is
bluesier and more soulful than Witchcraft's, closer to Led Zeppelin, Free and early Sabbath,
with Nilsson's raw, ragged voice.

The self-titled debut (2007) got attention. *Hisingen Blues* (2011), named after their part of
Gothenburg and released on Nuclear Blast, made them one of the biggest retro rock bands in
Europe. *Lights Out* (2012) and *Innocence & Decadence* (2015) followed. The band briefly split
in 2016, came back with *Peace* (2018), and released *6* (2023) **(verify)**.
