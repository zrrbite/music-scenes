# Witchcraft

- **Base:** Örebro, Sweden
- **Formed:** 2000
- **Key member:** Magnus Pelander (vocals, guitar, songwriter, the only constant)
- **Style:** Retro doom / occult heavy rock

## History
After his band [Norrsken](norrsken.md) ended, Magnus Pelander started Witchcraft partly as a tribute to his
heroes, Pentagram and Roky Erickson. The early records were recorded with deliberately vintage
gear and production, aiming for an authentic 1970s sound.

Lee Dorrian's Rise Above Records released the debut *Witchcraft* (2004), followed by *Firewood*
(2005) and *The Alchemist* (2007), which made them leaders of the Swedish retro rock revival.
After a lineup overhaul, *Legend* (2012, Nuclear Blast) was heavier and more modern, and
*Nucleus* (2016) was darker and more progressive. *Black Metal* (2020), despite its name, is
essentially a quiet, acoustic Pelander solo record.
