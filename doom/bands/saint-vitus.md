# Saint Vitus

- **Base:** Los Angeles
- **Formed:** 1979 (as Tyrant)
- **Key members:** Dave Chandler (guitar), Scott Reagers (vocals, early and later), Scott "Wino" Weinrich (vocals, 1986–1991 and 2008 on)

## History
Saint Vitus played slow, heavy, Sabbath-style doom at the height of LA's hardcore punk era
and somehow fit in there. Greg Ginn of Black Flag signed them to his punk label SST, and they
toured with punk bands while most metal bands got faster and slicker.

Wino, from Maryland doom band The Obsessed, joined on vocals for *Born Too Late* (1986), the
band's defining record and practically a doom manifesto. *Mournful Cries* (1988) and *V*
(1990) followed. The band has broken up and re-formed several times since, releasing *Lillie:
F-65* (2012) with Wino.
