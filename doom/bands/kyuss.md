# Kyuss

- **Base:** Palm Desert, California
- **Active:** 1987–1995
- **Members:** John Garcia (vocals), Josh Homme (guitar), Brant Bjork (drums, to 1993), Nick Oliveri (bass, early), Scott Reeder (bass, later)
- **Style:** Stoner / desert rock

## History
Kyuss grew up playing "generator parties", gigs in the desert powered by gas generators, far from
any club. Josh Homme played his guitar through bass amps, giving them an enormous, fuzzed-out,
hypnotic sound: Black Sabbath slowed into heat haze. *Blues for the Red Sun* (1992), produced by
Chris Goss, and *Welcome to Sky Valley* (1994) are the founding records of stoner rock.

The band split in 1995 after *...And the Circus Leaves Town*. Homme formed [Queens of the Stone
Age](../../seattle-grunge/bands/queens-of-the-stone-age.md), Brant Bjork joined Fu Manchu, and John
Garcia formed Unida. Their influence runs through every stoner band since.
