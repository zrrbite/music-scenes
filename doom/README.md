# Doom Metal & Retro Heavy Rock

Slow, heavy, Sabbath-worshipping music, from 70s originators through 80s cult bands and
90s stoner/sludge, to the Swedish retro wave of Witchcraft and Graveyard.

## History

### The origin (1968–1975)
Doom starts with **[Black Sabbath](bands/black-sabbath.md)**. Tony Iommi's slow, downtuned
riffs (he lost two fingertips in a factory accident and tuned down to ease the strain), the
tritone of the song "Black Sabbath", and occult and horror themes basically created heavy
metal. The slowest, heaviest parts of their first records are what doom would build on.

### The cult years (1970s–1980s)
While most of metal got faster in the 80s, a few bands stayed slow. In the US,
**[Pentagram](bands/pentagram.md)** (around since 1971 but barely recorded),
**[Saint Vitus](bands/saint-vitus.md)** (signed to the punk label SST) and Trouble (Chicago)
made "traditional doom": heavy, bluesy and depressive. In Sweden, **[Candlemass](bands/candlemass.md)**'
*Epicus Doomicus Metallicus* (1986) added operatic vocals and grand, classical riffs, gave the
genre its name, and started "epic doom".

### The 90s branches
- **Death/doom:** UK bands like Paradise Lost, My Dying Bride and Anathema (the "Peaceville
  three") merged doom with death metal and gothic atmosphere.
- **[Cathedral](bands/cathedral.md):** Lee Dorrian left grindcore pioneers Napalm Death to play
  the slowest music possible. His label **Rise Above Records** became the home of the doom
  underground for the next 30 years.
- **Stoner doom:** **[Sleep](bands/sleep.md)** in California and **[Electric
  Wizard](bands/electric-wizard.md)** in England made the riff a hazy, druggy ritual. Desert
  rock (Kyuss) and sludge (Eyehategod, Crowbar) grew in parallel.

### The Swedish retro wave (2000s – now)
In the 2000s a group of Swedish bands looked back past metal to the late-60s/early-70s
sound itself: warm analog production, bluesy riffs, and Pentagram and Sabbath worship.
Norrsken, a short-lived band from Örebro, split into two lines. Magnus Pelander formed
**[Witchcraft](bands/witchcraft.md)** (first signed to Rise Above), and Joakim Nilsson and
Rikard Edlund later formed **[Graveyard](bands/graveyard.md)** in Gothenburg. Blues Pills,
Ghost and many others followed in the same retro/occult rock revival.
