# Listening Guide

## Coming from Witchcraft and Graveyard
1. Witchcraft – *Firewood* (2005)
2. Graveyard – *Hisingen Blues* (2011)
3. Pentagram – *Relentless* (1985): the band Witchcraft was built around
4. Black Sabbath – *Master of Reality* (1971)
5. Saint Vitus – *Born Too Late* (1986)

## The essential path, from the origin onward
1. Black Sabbath – *Black Sabbath* (1970)
2. Pentagram – *Relentless* (1985)
3. Candlemass – *Epicus Doomicus Metallicus* (1986)
4. Cathedral – *Forest of Equilibrium* (1991)
5. Sleep – *Holy Mountain* (1992)
6. Electric Wizard – *Dopethrone* (2000)

## Warm and retro
- Witchcraft – *Witchcraft* (2004), *The Alchemist* (2007)
- Graveyard – *Graveyard* (2007), *Lights Out* (2012)
- Cathedral – *The Ethereal Mirror* (1993)

## Slowest and heaviest
- Sleep – *Dopesmoker*
- Electric Wizard – *Dopethrone* (2000)
- Cathedral – *Forest of Equilibrium* (1991)

## Epic and dramatic
- Candlemass – *Epicus Doomicus Metallicus* (1986), *Nightfall* (1987)
