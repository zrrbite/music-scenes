# NOFX

- **Base:** Los Angeles (later San Francisco)
- **Active:** 1983–2024 **(verify end)**
- **Members:** Fat Mike (Mike Burkett, bass/vocals), Eric Melvin (guitar), Erik Sandin ("Smelly", drums), El Hefe (Aaron Abeyta, guitar/trumpet, from 1991)

## History
NOFX started in LA in 1983 as a sloppy hardcore band and turned into the archetypal skate-punk
band: fast, melodic, funny, offensive and very self-mocking, with occasional ska and reggae
parts and El Hefe's trumpet. In 1990 Fat Mike and his then-wife Erin founded **Fat Wreck
Chords**, which became, with Epitaph, the center of 90s punk.

*Punk in Drublic* (1994, Epitaph), with "Linoleum" and "Leave It Alone", went gold with no major
label, no radio push and the band refusing MTV videos. *The Decline* (1999), a single 18-minute
song about American politics, is often called their masterpiece. Later records like *The War on
Errorism* (2003) were openly political. The band announced a farewell tour for its 40th
anniversary and played its final shows in 2024 **(verify)**.
