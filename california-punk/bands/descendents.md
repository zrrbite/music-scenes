# Descendents

- **Base:** Manhattan Beach, Los Angeles
- **Formed:** 1977
- **Key members:** Milo Aukerman (vocals), Bill Stevenson (drums), Karl Alvarez (bass), Stephen Egerton (guitar)

## History
The Descendents came out of the same South Bay scene as Black Flag. Drummer Bill Stevenson
played in both. They paired fast hardcore with catchy melodies and lyrics about girls, coffee,
food and being a misfit. *Milo Goes to College* (1982), with its cartoon cover of the nerdy,
bespectacled Milo, became the founding document of pop-punk. The title was literal: Milo went
off to college, and later became a biochemist.

The band recorded on and off around Milo's studies (*I Don't Want to Grow Up*, 1985; *All*,
1987). Without Milo, the others continued as ALL. Milo came back for *Everything Sucks* (1996),
and the band has recorded occasionally since, for example *Hypercaffium Spazzinate* (2016).
Stevenson's Blasting Room studio in Colorado became a major punk recording studio. Green Day,
Blink-182 and countless others name the Descendents as a key influence.
