# Rancid

- **Base:** Berkeley, California
- **Formed:** 1991
- **Members:** Tim Armstrong (vocals/guitar), Matt Freeman (bass/vocals), Lars Frederiksen (guitar/vocals, from 1993), Brett Reed (drums, until 2006), Branden Steineckert (drums, from 2006)

## History
Tim Armstrong and Matt Freeman had been in Operation Ivy, the Gilman Street ska-punk band that
split in 1989. They formed Rancid in 1991, took in the Clash, Oi! and ska, and signed to
Epitaph. *Let's Go* (1994) caught the punk boom, and major labels fought over the band. They
turned them all down, reportedly including an offer from Madonna's label, and stayed with
Epitaph.

*...And Out Come the Wolves* (1995), with "Time Bomb", "Ruby Soho" and "Roots Radicals", is their
classic. Armstrong started Hellcat Records, an Epitaph sub-label, and later the hip-hop/punk
project Transplants with Travis Barker.
