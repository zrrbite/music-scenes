# Dead Kennedys

- **Base:** San Francisco
- **Formed:** 1978. Jello Biafra left in 1986; the others re-formed without him from 2001.
- **Members:** Jello Biafra (vocals), East Bay Ray (guitar), Klaus Flouride (bass), D.H. Peligro (drums, d. 2022); first drummer Ted

## History
The leading band of San Francisco punk. East Bay Ray's surf-tinged, reverb-heavy guitar and
Jello Biafra's sarcastic, quavering voice carried savagely funny political satire. *Fresh Fruit for
Rotting Vegetables* (1980), with "California Über Alles", "Holiday in Cambodia" and "Kill the Poor",
is one of the essential punk albums. Biafra also ran for mayor of San Francisco in 1979 and came fourth.

Biafra founded **Alternative Tentacles Records**, which later released records by Slim Cessna's
Auto Club in the [gothic Americana](../../gothic-americana/README.md) section. *Frankenchrist*
(1985) came with a poster of an H. R. Giger painting, which led to an obscenity prosecution
against Biafra. The case ended in a hung jury, but it drained the band, and they split in 1986.
Later legal disputes between Biafra and the others were bitter.
