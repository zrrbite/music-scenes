# The Distillers

- **Base:** Los Angeles
- **Formed:** 1998 (inactive 2006–2018, then reunited)
- **Key member:** Brody Dalle (vocals/guitar)

## History
Australian-born Brody Dalle formed The Distillers in LA. Her raw, ragged growl is the band's
trademark. Dalle was married to Tim Armstrong of [Rancid](rancid.md), and the first two albums came
out on his Epitaph sub-label Hellcat: *The Distillers* (2000) and *Sing Sing Death House* (2002).
*Coral Fang* (2003) went to a major label and moved toward a heavier, grungier sound.

Dalle later married Josh Homme of [Queens of the Stone Age](../../seattle-grunge/bands/queens-of-the-stone-age.md).
She formed Spinnerette and made a solo album before reviving The Distillers in 2018.
