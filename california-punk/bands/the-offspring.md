# The Offspring

- **Base:** Garden Grove / Huntington Beach, Orange County, California
- **Formed:** 1984 (as Manic Subsidal; renamed 1986)
- **Key members:** Dexter Holland (vocals/guitar), Noodles (Kevin Wasserman, guitar), Greg K. (bass, until 2018), Ron Welty (drums, 1987–2003); later drummers include Atom Willard, Pete Parada and Brandon Pertzborn **(verify)**

## History
Dexter Holland and Greg K. started the band in high school in Orange County. Noodles, older
than the others and working as a school janitor, joined because he was old enough to buy them
beer. The self-titled debut (1989) came out on a tiny label. *Ignition* (1992) got them signed
to Epitaph. Holland was meanwhile working toward a PhD in molecular biology, which he finally
completed in 2017.

*Smash* (April 1994), with "Come Out and Play" ("You gotta keep 'em separated"), "Self Esteem"
and "Gotta Get Away", exploded on alternative radio and MTV. It sold over 10 million copies
worldwide and became the best-selling album ever released on an independent label. It also made
Epitaph a powerhouse.

After a dispute with Epitaph, the band moved to Columbia for *Ixnay on the Hombre* (1997).
*Americana* (1998), with "Pretty Fly (for a White Guy)", "The Kids Aren't Alright" and "Why Don't
You Get a Job?", was another massive hit. They've kept recording and touring ever since:
*Conspiracy of One* (2000), *Splinter* (2003), *Rise and Fall, Rage and Grace* (2008, with "You're
Gonna Go Far, Kid"), *Let the Bad Times Roll* (2021) and *Supercharged* (2024) **(verify)**.
