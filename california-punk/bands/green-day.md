# Green Day

- **Base:** East Bay (Berkeley / Rodeo area), California
- **Formed:** 1987 (as Sweet Children)
- **Members:** Billie Joe Armstrong (vocals/guitar), Mike Dirnt (bass), Tré Cool (drums, from 1990)

## History
Billie Joe Armstrong and Mike Dirnt were childhood friends in the East Bay. As Green Day they
became regulars at 924 Gilman Street and released *39/Smooth* (1990) and *Kerplunk* (1991) on
Lookout! Records. Tré Cool joined on drums in 1990.

They signed to Reprise, and *Dookie* (February 1994), produced by Rob Cavallo, with "Longview",
"Basket Case" and "When I Come Around", sold over 10 million copies in the US alone. A
mud-fight set at Woodstock '94 sealed their fame. Gilman Street banned them for signing to a
major. *Insomniac* (1995) was darker and *Nimrod* (1997) included the acoustic "Good Riddance
(Time of Your Life)".

After a slump, *American Idiot* (2004), a punk rock opera about post-9/11 America, was an
enormous comeback. It later became a Broadway musical. *21st Century Breakdown* (2009) continued
in that vein, and they've kept releasing albums into the 2020s.
