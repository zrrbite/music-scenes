# Sublime

- **Base:** Long Beach, California
- **Formed:** 1988
- **Members:** Bradley Nowell (vocals/guitar, d. 1996), Eric Wilson (bass), Bud Gaugh (drums); since 2023 with Nowell's son Jakob Nowell on vocals **(verify)**

## History
Sublime mixed punk, ska, reggae, dub and hip-hop into a laid-back Southern California sound.
They built a huge following through local shows and self-released tapes on their own label,
Skunk Records. *40oz. to Freedom* (1992) and *Robbin' the Hood* (1994) spread by word of mouth,
and they played the early Warped Tours.

Bradley Nowell died of a heroin overdose in May 1996, two months before the self-titled major-label
album came out. *Sublime* (1996), with "What I Got", "Santeria" and "Wrong Way", became a massive hit
anyway. Wilson and Gaugh continued as Sublime with Rome. In 2023 they reunited as Sublime with
Jakob Nowell.
