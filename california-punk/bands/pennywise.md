# Pennywise

- **Base:** Hermosa Beach, Los Angeles
- **Formed:** 1988
- **Key members:** Jim Lindberg (vocals), Fletcher Dragge (guitar), Byron McMackin (drums), Jason Thirsk (bass, d. 1996), Randy Bradbury (bass, from 1996)

## History
Pennywise came from the South Bay surf and skate scene, the same beach towns as Black Flag.
They played fast, anthemic melodic hardcore with positive, defiant lyrics. Signed to Epitaph,
they released *Pennywise* (1991), *Unknown Road* (1993) and *About Time* (1995) and became
one of the label's biggest acts.

Founding bassist Jason Thirsk died in 1996. *Full Circle* (1997) included "Bro Hymn Tribute",
a re-recording of their song "Bro Hymn" dedicated to him, which became the band's singalong
anthem at every show. They've stayed a fixture of the scene and Warped Tour ever since.
