# Social Distortion

- **Base:** Fullerton, Orange County, California
- **Formed:** 1978
- **Key members:** Mike Ness (vocals/guitar, the constant), Dennis Danell (guitar, d. 2000)

## History
Social Distortion were early Orange County punks. *Mommy's Little Monster* (1983) is a classic of
the scene. Mike Ness' heroin addiction then put the band on hold for years. When he got sober,
the music changed: *Prison Bound* (1988) brought in country and rockabilly, and *Social
Distortion* (1990), with "Ball and Chain", "Story of My Life" and a cover of Johnny Cash's "Ring
of Fire", made them a major-label success.

*Somewhere Between Heaven and Hell* (1992) and *White Light, White Heat, White Trash* (1996)
followed. Co-founder Dennis Danell died in 2000. Ness released solo records of country and
roots covers, and the band has carried on with *Sex, Love and Rock 'n' Roll* (2004) and *Hard
Times and Nursery Rhymes* (2011). They link punk with the darker American roots music in the
[gothic Americana](../../gothic-americana/README.md) section.
