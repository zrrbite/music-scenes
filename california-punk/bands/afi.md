# AFI

- **Base:** Ukiah, Northern California
- **Formed:** 1991
- **Members:** Davey Havok (vocals), Jade Puget (guitar, from 1998), Hunter Burgan (bass), Adam Carson (drums)

## History
AFI (A Fire Inside) started as teenage hardcore punk. Their early records came out on **Nitro
Records**, the label The Offspring's Dexter Holland and Greg K. co-founded in 1994: *Answer That
and Stay Fashionable* (1995), *Very Proud of Ya* (1996), *Black Sails in the Sunset* (1999) and *The Art
of Drowning* (2000). Over that run the music turned darker, more melodic and more gothic, with
Havok's theatrical, horror-inspired image to match.

They signed to a major for *Sing the Sorrow* (2003), with "Girl's Not Grey" and "The Leaving Song
Pt. II", a big crossover success. *Decemberunderground* (2006), with "Miss Murder", took them further
into goth-rock and electronics. Havok and Puget also make synth-pop as Blaqk Audio.
