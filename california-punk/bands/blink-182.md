# Blink-182

- **Base:** Poway, San Diego, California
- **Formed:** 1992
- **Members:** Mark Hoppus (bass/vocals), Tom DeLonge (guitar/vocals; away 2005–2009 and 2015–2022), Scott Raynor (drums, until 1998), Travis Barker (drums, from 1998); Matt Skiba of Alkaline Trio (guitar/vocals, 2015–2022)

## History
Tom DeLonge and Mark Hoppus formed the band in the San Diego suburbs, playing fast, goofy,
catchy pop-punk in the Descendents mold. *Cheshire Cat* (1995) and *Dude Ranch* (1997, with
"Dammit") built a following on the skate-punk circuit. Travis Barker of The Aquabats joined in
1998, and his drumming changed the band.

*Enema of the State* (1999), with "What's My Age Again?", "All the Small Things" and "Adam's
Song", made them one of the biggest bands in the world and set the template for 2000s pop-punk.
*Take Off Your Pants and Jacket* (2001) and the darker, more experimental self-titled album (2003)
followed. They went on hiatus in 2005, which produced Angels & Airwaves and +44. They reunited
in 2009, Matt Skiba replaced DeLonge in 2015, and DeLonge returned in 2022 for *One More Time...*
(2023).
