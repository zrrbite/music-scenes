# Bad Religion

- **Base:** San Fernando Valley, Los Angeles
- **Formed:** 1980
- **Key members:** Greg Graffin (vocals), Brett Gurewitz (guitar, songwriter), Jay Bentley (bass), Greg Hetson (guitar, until 2013), Brian Baker (guitar, from 1994; ex-Minor Threat)

## History
Teenagers Greg Graffin and Brett Gurewitz started Bad Religion in 1980. Gurewitz founded
Epitaph Records just to release their records. *How Could Hell Be Any Worse?* (1982) was a hit
in the LA scene. Then came *Into the Unknown* (1983), a synth-heavy progressive rock detour that
fans hated, and the band nearly fell apart.

They reunited for *Suffer* (1988), which defined melodic hardcore: very fast, very short songs,
the trademark three-part backing harmonies, and dense, vocabulary-heavy lyrics about society,
religion and science. *No Control* (1989), *Against the Grain* (1990), *Generator* (1992) and *Recipe
for Hate* (1993) followed. Graffin earned a PhD in zoology/evolutionary biology and has lectured
on evolution.

They moved to Atlantic for *Stranger Than Fiction* (1994), with "Infected" and "21st Century
(Digital Boy)". That year Gurewitz left the band to run Epitaph, which was exploding thanks to
The Offspring. Brian Baker of Minor Threat replaced him. Gurewitz returned for *The Process of
Belief* (2002), and the band has stayed active since.
