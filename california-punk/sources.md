# Sources

## Accuracy notes
Written from general knowledge and not yet checked against references. The major events
(1994, Epitaph and *Smash*, Gilman Street) are very well documented. Recent lineup changes and
end dates are the least certain and are marked **(verify)**.

## Good references
- *The Decline of Western Civilization* (1981), Penelope Spheeris' documentary on LA punk
- *We Got the Neutron Bomb: The Untold Story of L.A. Punk* by Marc Spitz and Brendan Mullen
- *Our Band Could Be Your Life* by Michael Azerrad (Black Flag and SST chapters)
- *Filmage: The Story of Descendents/All* (2013) documentary
- *NOFX: The Hepatitis Bathtub and Other Stories* (band memoir)
