# Revocation

- **Base:** Boston, Massachusetts
- **Formed:** 2006 (from Cryptic Warning, 2000)
- **Key members:** Dave Davidson (guitar/vocals, founder); drummer Ash Pearson (ex-3 Inches of Blood) from about 2015 **(verify)**
- **Style:** Technical death / thrash metal with jazz-influenced guitar

## History
Dave Davidson studied at Berklee College of Music, and it shows. Revocation combine thrash
riffs, technical death metal and jazz-influenced harmony and soloing, with a sense of melody
that recalls late-era Death. As Cryptic Warning, the band played thrash. Renamed Revocation,
they self-released *Empire of the Obscene* (2008) and then signed to Relapse for *Existence Is
Futile* (2009) and *Chaos of Forms* (2011).

They moved to Metal Blade for *Deathless* (2014), and *Great Is Our Sin* (2016) followed. *The
Outer Ones* (2018) was a Lovecraft-themed record and a step toward pure death metal, and
*Netherheaven* (2022) went darker still. Davidson is one of the most respected guitarists in
modern metal.
