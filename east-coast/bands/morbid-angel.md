# Morbid Angel

- **Base:** Tampa, Florida
- **Formed:** 1983
- **Key members:** Trey Azagthoth (guitar, founder), David Vincent (bass/vocals), Pete Sandoval (drums), Steve Tucker (bass/vocals, at other times)

## History
Trey Azagthoth's Morbid Angel brought otherworldly, chaotic guitar solos, occult and
Lovecraftian themes, and very fast drumming to death metal. *Altars of Madness* (1989) is one
of the genre's foundational albums. Their albums famously run in alphabetical order:
*Blessed Are the Sick* (1991), *Covenant* (1993), *Domination* (1995), and so on.

*Covenant* came out on a major label (Giant/Warner), a first for death metal. Steve Tucker
replaced David Vincent for *Formulas Fatal to the Flesh* (1998) and *Gateways to
Annihilation* (2000). Vincent's return on *Illud Divinum Insanus* (2011), with its industrial
experiments, was badly received. Tucker returned for *Kingdoms Disdained* (2017).
