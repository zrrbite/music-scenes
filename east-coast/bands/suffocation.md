# Suffocation

- **Base:** Centereach, Long Island, New York
- **Formed:** 1988
- **Key members:** Frank Mullen (vocals, until about 2018), Terrance Hobbs (guitar), Mike Smith (drums), Derek Boyer (bass, later)

## History
Suffocation invented brutal death metal. Their songs combine very technical, dense riffing
and blast beats with sudden, crushing slow sections, the "slam" breakdowns that later inspired
slam and deathcore. Frank Mullen's deep growl set the standard for the vocal style.

After the *Human Waste* EP (1991), they recorded *Effigy of the Forgotten* (1991) with Scott
Burns at Morrisound for Roadrunner. *Breeding the Spawn* (1993), *Pierced from Within*
(1995) and the *Despise the Sun* EP (1998) followed. The band split in 1998, reunited in 2002
with *Souls to Deny* (2004), and has kept going since. Mullen retired from touring around
2018 and later from the band.

Also central to [Brutal death, slam & deathcore](../../brutal-death/README.md).
