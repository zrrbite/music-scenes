# Nile

- **Base:** Greenville, South Carolina
- **Formed:** 1993
- **Key members:** Karl Sanders (guitar/vocals, founder), Dallas Toler-Wade (guitar/vocals, to 2016), George Kollias (drums, from 2004)
- **Style:** Technical brutal death metal on ancient Egyptian themes

## History
Karl Sanders built Nile around his obsession with ancient Egypt and Near Eastern mythology.
Very fast, technical brutal death metal sits alongside Middle Eastern scales and instruments,
chants, and long, scholarly liner notes explaining the lyrics. *Amongst the Catacombs of Nephren-Ka*
(1998), *Black Seeds of Vengeance* (2000) and *In Their Darkened Shrines* (2002) set the style.

With Greek drummer George Kollias, one of the fastest in the genre, *Annihilation of the Wicked*
(2005), *Ithyphallic* (2007) and later albums kept them among the top tech-death bands. Their use
of Middle Eastern melody makes them a cousin to Quebec City's [Aeternam](../../quebec/bands/aeternam.md).
