# Death

- **Base:** Orlando area, later Tampa, Florida
- **Active:** 1983–2001 (began as Mantas)
- **Key member:** Chuck Schuldiner (guitar/vocals, songwriter, d. 2001)

## History
Chuck Schuldiner started Mantas as a teenager in 1983 and renamed it Death. After a brief
spell in San Francisco and Toronto, he recorded *Scream Bloody Gore* (1987) with drummer Chris
Reifert. It's often called the first true death metal album. *Leprosy* (1988) and *Spiritual
Healing* (1990) followed. Chuck is often called "the father of death metal".

Chuck kept rebuilding the band with new musicians, and the music kept getting more
sophisticated. *Human* (1991), with Cynic's Paul Masvidal and Sean Reinert and bassist Steve
DiGiorgio, introduced jazz-trained technicality. *Individual Thought Patterns* (1993, with
Gene Hoglan and Andy LaRocque), *Symbolic* (1995) and *The Sound of Perseverance* (1998) made
Death the template for technical and progressive death metal. His lyrics moved from gore to
introspection and social commentary.

Chuck was diagnosed with a brain tumor and died in December 2001 at 34. Every technical
death metal band in this repo, from Revocation to Beyond Creation, owes him something.
