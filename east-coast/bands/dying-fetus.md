# Dying Fetus

- **Base:** Upper Marlboro, Maryland
- **Formed:** 1991
- **Key members:** John Gallagher (guitar/vocals, founder), Sean Beasley (bass/vocals), Trey Williams (drums)

## History
Dying Fetus combined brutal death metal with grindcore and hardcore-style mosh grooves. The
result is often called the bridge between death metal and the bouncy breakdowns of deathcore.
*Killing on Adrenaline* (1998) and *Destroy the Opposition* (2000) are the classics.

In 2001 most of the lineup left. Jason Netherton and Sparky Voyles formed Misery Index, and
Gallagher rebuilt the band. Since then, a stable trio has released albums like *Stop at
Nothing* (2003) and *Wrong One to Fuck With* (2017). Their politically angry lyrics stand out
from the usual gore.

Also central to [Brutal death, slam & deathcore](../../brutal-death/README.md).
