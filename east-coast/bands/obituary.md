# Obituary

- **Base:** Tampa, Florida
- **Formed:** 1984 (as Executioner / Xecutioner)
- **Key members:** John Tardy (vocals), Donald Tardy (drums), Trevor Peres (guitar)

## History
Where Morbid Angel went fast, Obituary went slow. Their heavy, groovy death metal took a lot
from Celtic Frost, and John Tardy's vocals are almost wordless, guttural howls. *Slowly We Rot*
(1989) and *Cause of Death* (1990, with guitarist James Murphy and a Celtic Frost cover) are
classics of the Florida sound. *The End Complete* (1992) was their best seller.

The band has continued with steady lineups, independent releases and crowdfunding. They're
a big influence on death/doom and on the groovier side of death metal.
