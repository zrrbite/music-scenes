# Cannibal Corpse

- **Base:** formed in Buffalo, New York; later based in Tampa, Florida
- **Formed:** 1988
- **Key members:** Alex Webster (bass), Paul Mazurkiewicz (drums), Chris Barnes (vocals, until 1995), George "Corpsegrinder" Fisher (vocals, from 1995), Rob Barrett (guitar), Erik Rutan (guitar, since about 2019)

## History
Cannibal Corpse formed in Buffalo and moved to Tampa to be near the Florida scene and
Morrisound. With Chris Barnes they released *Eaten Back to Life* (1990), *Butchered at Birth*
(1991), *Tomb of the Mutilated* (1992) and *The Bleeding* (1994). Their gore lyrics and
covers got them banned or censored in several countries for years. Jim Carrey, a fan, put
them in a cameo in *Ace Ventura: Pet Detective* (1994).

Barnes was fired in 1995 and formed Six Feet Under. George "Corpsegrinder" Fisher from
Monstrosity took over with *Vile* (1996), and the band has released albums on Metal Blade
steadily ever since. They're widely reported to be the best-selling death metal band ever.
Alex Webster's virtuosic bass playing is a highlight.
