# Immolation

- **Base:** Yonkers, New York
- **Formed:** 1986 (from Rigor Mortis)
- **Key members:** Ross Dolan (bass/vocals), Robert Vigna (guitar), Steve Shalaty (drums, later)

## History
Immolation made dark, dissonant, anti-religious death metal, with Robert Vigna's strange,
twisting riffs and wild physical stage presence. *Dawn of Possession* (1991, Roadrunner) is a
classic, and *Here in After* (1996), *Failures for Gods* (1999) and *Close to a World Below*
(2000) developed a uniquely suffocating atmosphere. *Close to a World Below* is often named
their best.

They've been remarkably consistent, with later albums like *Majesty and Decay* (2010),
*Atonement* (2017) and *Acts of God* (2022). They're a parallel to Gorguts in bringing
dissonance into death metal, and a major influence on today's "dissonant death metal" bands.
