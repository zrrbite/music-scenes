# US East Coast Death Metal

The two great American death metal centers on the Atlantic side: **Florida**, where the
first wave was born, and **New York**, where brutal death metal came from. Plus the
technical bands that grew out of both, up to Boston's Revocation.

## History

### Florida: Morrisound and the first wave (1980s – early 1990s)
In the mid-80s Florida became the center of death metal. Chuck Schuldiner's
**[Death](bands/death.md)** released *Scream Bloody Gore* (1987), and in Tampa
**[Morbid Angel](bands/morbid-angel.md)** and **[Obituary](bands/obituary.md)** defined the
sound: Morbid Angel fast and occult, Obituary slow and sludgy. Nearly everyone recorded at
**Morrisound Studios** in Tampa with producer **Scott Burns**, which gave the scene a
consistent, powerful sound. Deicide, Monstrosity and Malevolent Creation were part of the same
scene, and Buffalo's **[Cannibal Corpse](bands/cannibal-corpse.md)** eventually moved there.

In the early 90s Florida also produced the progressive turn: Death's *Human* (1991),
with members of Miami's Cynic, and Sarasota's Atheist pushed death metal toward jazz fusion.
That lineage leads straight to [Quebec](../quebec/README.md)'s technical scene.

### New York: brutality (late 1980s – 1990s)
Up north, **[Suffocation](bands/suffocation.md)** from Long Island took death metal to a new
extreme: very technical riffing, blast beats, and the slow, crushing breakdowns that later
inspired slam and deathcore. *Effigy of the Forgotten* (1991) is the foundation of brutal death
metal. **[Immolation](bands/immolation.md)** from Yonkers went darker and more dissonant, a
parallel line to Gorguts. Further south, Maryland's **[Dying Fetus](bands/dying-fetus.md)**
mixed brutal death with grind and hardcore-style grooves.

### The modern technical side (2000s – now)
**[Revocation](bands/revocation.md)**, from Boston, combine technical death metal, thrash,
and jazz-influenced guitar playing from Berklee-trained Dave Davidson. They carry Death's
progressive spirit into the present.
