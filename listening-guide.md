# Listening Guide

Pick a lane and work outward.

## "I want the classics"
1. Gorguts – *Obscura* (1998)
2. Cryptopsy – *None So Vile* (1996)
3. Gorguts – *The Erosion of Sanity* (1993)
4. Martyr – *Warp Zone* (2000)

## "Give me the modern tech-prog sound" (Beyond Creation / First Fragment benchmark)
1. Beyond Creation – *The Aura* (2011)
2. Beyond Creation – *Earthborn Evolution* (2014)
3. First Fragment – *Gloire Éternelle* (2021)
4. Augury – *Fragmentary Evidence* (2009)
5. Sutrah – *Dunes*

### Bright and neoclassical vs. cosmic and atmospheric
- **Bright / neoclassical / shred:** First Fragment → Quo Vadis → Martyr
- **Cosmic / atmospheric / bass-forward:** Beyond Creation → Augury → Sutrah

## "Melodic but still technical"
1. Quo Vadis – *Defiant Imagination* (2004)
2. Neuraxis – *Trilateral Progression* (2005)
3. Aeternam – *Moongates Guardian* (2014)

## "Weird, dark, avant-garde"
1. Gorguts – *Colored Sands* (2013)
2. Chthe'ilist – *Le Dernier Crépuscule* (2016)
3. Unexpect – *In a Flesh Aquarium* (2006)

## "Just make it heavy"
1. Cryptopsy – *Blasphemy Made Flesh* (1994)
2. Kataklysm – *Sorcery* (1995)
3. Despised Icon – *The Ills of Modern Man* (2007)
4. Beneath the Massacre – *Dystopia* (2008)
