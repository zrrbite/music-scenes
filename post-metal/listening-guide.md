# Listening Guide

These albums work best heard in full, loud, in one sitting.

## The essential path
1. Neurosis – *Through Silver in Blood* (1996)
2. Isis – *Oceanic* (2002)
3. Cult of Luna – *Somewhere Along the Highway* (2006)
4. The Ocean – *Pelagial* (2013)
5. Amenra – *Mass VI* (2017)

## Coming from metal
- Cult of Luna – *Vertikal* (2013)
- The Ocean – *Phanerozoic I: Palaeozoic* (2018)
- Neurosis – *Times of Grace* (1999)

## Coming from post-rock
- Isis – *Panopticon* (2004)
- Russian Circles – *Station* (2008)
- Cult of Luna & Julie Christmas – *Mariner* (2016)

## Heaviest and most harrowing
- Neurosis – *Enemy of the Sun* (1993)
- Amenra – *Mass V* (2012)
