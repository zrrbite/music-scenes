# Mastodon

- **Base:** Atlanta, Georgia
- **Formed:** 2000
- **Members:** Troy Sanders (bass/vocals), Brent Hinds (guitar/vocals, until 2025 **(verify)**), Bill Kelliher (guitar), Brann Dailor (drums/vocals)

## History
Mastodon started as a sludge band in the Neurosis tradition and grew into one of the defining
progressive metal bands of the 21st century. *Remission* (2002) was raw and heavy. *Leviathan*
(2004), a concept album based on *Moby-Dick*, was the breakthrough. *Blood Mountain* (2006) and
*Crack the Skye* (2009), a dense prog epic touching on Rasputin, astral travel and Brann Dailor's
late sister, are often called their best.

Neurosis' Scott Kelly sang guest vocals on many of their albums. Later records like *The Hunter*
(2011), *Emperor of Sand* (2017) and *Hushed and Grim* (2021) brought in more melody and clean
singing. Brent Hinds left in 2025 and died later that year **(verify)**.
