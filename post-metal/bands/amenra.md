# Amenra

- **Base:** Kortrijk, Belgium
- **Formed:** 1999
- **Key member:** Colin H. van Eeckhout (vocals)

## History
Amenra makes music as ritual. Their albums are called *Mass* (*Mass I* to *Mass VI*), and their
live shows are famously intense: van Eeckhout often performs with his back to the audience,
and the music swings between whispered calm and overwhelming, sludgy catharsis. Themes of
pain, grief and spiritual purification run through all of it. The band is the core of the
Church of Ra, a wider collective of related Belgian artists.

*Mass V* (2012) and *Mass VI* (2017, Neurot Recordings **(verify)**) brought them international
recognition. *De Doorn* (2021) was their first album sung entirely in Flemish/Dutch.
