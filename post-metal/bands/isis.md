# Isis

- **Base:** Boston, later Los Angeles
- **Active:** 1997–2010
- **Key members:** Aaron Turner (vocals/guitar), Jeff Caxide (bass), Aaron Harris (drums), Michael Gallagher (guitar), Bryant Clifford Meyer (keyboards/guitar)

## History
Isis began in Boston as a heavy, Neurosis-indebted sludge band (*Celestial*, 2000). With
*Oceanic* (2002) they found a new form: long, slowly unfolding songs that build from clean,
almost post-rock passages to crushing climaxes, with Turner's roar used sparingly. That record
largely defined "post-metal". *Panopticon* (2004), themed around surveillance, is just as
important, and *In the Absence of Truth* (2006) and *Wavering Radiant* (2009) refined the sound further.

The band moved to Los Angeles and broke up in 2010 at the height of its popularity, saying
it had done what it set out to do. Turner's label Hydra Head and his many later projects
(Old Man Gloom, Sumac, Mamiffer) remain central to heavy experimental music.
