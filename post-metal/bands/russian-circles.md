# Russian Circles

- **Base:** Chicago
- **Formed:** 2004
- **Members:** Mike Sullivan (guitar), Dave Turncrantz (drums), Brian Cook (bass, from 2007; ex-Botch)
- **Style:** Instrumental post-metal

## History
A trio with no vocalist. Sullivan builds layered guitar loops live, over heavy, precise drumming
from Turncrantz. *Enter* (2006) introduced them. *Station* (2008), Brian Cook's first album with
the band, includes "Harper Lewis" and showed off their mix of post-rock melody and metal weight.
*Geneva* (2009), *Empros* (2011), *Memorial* (2013) and *Guidance* (2016) followed, and they've
toured widely, including with Cult of Luna and others.
