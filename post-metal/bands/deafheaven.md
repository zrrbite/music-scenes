# Deafheaven

- **Base:** San Francisco
- **Formed:** 2010
- **Key members:** George Clarke (vocals), Kerry McCoy (guitar)
- **Style:** "Blackgaze": black metal combined with shoegaze and post-rock

## History
Deafheaven built on the blackgaze of France's Alcest: black metal's blast beats and shrieks
under the shimmering, wall-of-sound guitars of shoegaze and post-rock. *Roads to Judah* (2011)
introduced them. *Sunbather* (2013), released on Deathwish Inc. with its bright pink cover, became
one of the most acclaimed and debated metal albums of the decade. It won over indie fans and
annoyed black metal purists.

*New Bermuda* (2015) was heavier, and *Ordinary Corrupt Human Love* (2018) more melodic. *Infinite
Granite* (2021) dropped the screams almost entirely, and *Lonely People with Power* (2025) **(verify)**
brought them back.
