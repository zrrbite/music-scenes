# Cult of Luna

- **Base:** Umeå, Sweden
- **Formed:** 1998
- **Key member:** Johannes Persson (guitar/vocals); the lineup has at times included two drummers

## History
Cult of Luna formed in Umeå, the northern Swedish town Meshuggah also comes from, out of
the local hardcore scene. Early records were heavy and sludgy. *Salvation* (2004) and
*Somewhere Along the Highway* (2006) developed their signature: enormous, slow crescendos,
layers of guitar, and a cold, cinematic atmosphere. *Eternal Kingdom* (2008) was built around
a fictional story.

*Vertikal* (2013) was inspired by Fritz Lang's film *Metropolis*, with a more mechanical,
synth-touched sound. *Mariner* (2016), made with American singer Julie Christmas, is a space-travel
concept album and a fan favorite. *A Dawn to Fear* (2019) and *The Long Road North* (2022)
returned to a raw, emotional heaviness.
