# Neurosis

- **Base:** Oakland, California
- **Formed:** 1985
- **Key members:** Steve Von Till (guitar/vocals), Scott Kelly (guitar/vocals, until 2022), Dave Edwardson (bass), Jason Roeder (drums), Noah Landis (keyboards/samples)

## History
Neurosis came out of the East Bay hardcore punk scene around the 924 Gilman Street club. By
*The Word as Law* (1990) they were already stretching out. *Souls at Zero* (1992) and *Enemy
of the Sun* (1993) took them into new territory: slow, tribal drumming, samples, keyboards,
and songs that build into apocalyptic peaks.

*Through Silver in Blood* (1996) is their masterpiece and the founding document of
post-metal. *Times of Grace* (1999), recorded by Steve Albini, came with a companion album by
their side project Tribes of Neurot designed to be played at the same time. Later albums like
*The Eye of Every Storm* (2004), *Given to the Rising* (2007) and *Honor Found in Decay* (2012)
grew more patient and spacious. They started their own label, Neurot Recordings, and are known
for rare but overwhelming live shows. Scott Kelly left the band in 2022.
