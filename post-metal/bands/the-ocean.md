# The Ocean (The Ocean Collective)

- **Base:** Berlin, Germany
- **Formed:** 2000
- **Key members:** Robin Staps (guitar, founder, main songwriter), Loïc Rossetti (vocals, since about 2009)
- **Style:** Progressive post-metal and sludge with orchestral parts

## History
Robin Staps started The Ocean as a rotating collective of musicians and built it around huge
concept albums, often with orchestral strings and brass. Early on, dozens of people
contributed. The double album *Precambrian* (2007) is named after the geological eon, and each
track is named for a geological period. *Heliocentric* and *Anthropocentric* (both 2010)
were about the conflict between science and religion.

*Pelagial* (2013) is their best-known work: one continuous piece that descends from the surface
of the ocean to the deep sea floor, getting heavier and darker with each zone. The two
*Phanerozoic* albums (2018, 2020) returned to geology, covering the last 540 million years of
the history of life. *Holocene* (2023) **(verify)** followed. Staps also runs the label Pelagic Records.
