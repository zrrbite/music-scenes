# Tool

- **Base:** Los Angeles
- **Formed:** 1990
- **Members:** Maynard James Keenan (vocals), Adam Jones (guitar), Danny Carey (drums), Justin Chancellor (bass, from 1995; replaced Paul D'Amour)

## Why it's in this section
Tool's long, patient, polyrhythmic songs and their mix of heaviness and mysticism made them the
most popular band in the wider progressive/post-metal world. Isis toured with them **(verify)**,
and a lot of post-metal fans started with Tool.

## History
*Undertow* (1993) was heavy alternative metal. With Justin Chancellor on bass, *Ænima* (1996) and
*Lateralus* (2001) developed their signature: odd time signatures (the title track of *Lateralus*
is built on the Fibonacci sequence), long builds, tribal drumming from Danny Carey, and Adam Jones'
surreal visual art and videos. *10,000 Days* (2006) followed.

After a famously long gap, *Fear Inoculum* (2019) went straight to number one. Keenan also leads
A Perfect Circle and Puscifer, and runs a winery in Arizona.
