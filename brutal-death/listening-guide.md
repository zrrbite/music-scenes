# Listening Guide

## The lineage, in order
1. Suffocation – *Effigy of the Forgotten* (1991): where it all comes from
2. Dying Fetus – *Destroy the Opposition* (2000): the groove
3. Devourment – *Molesting the Decapitated* (1999): the slam
4. Aborted – *Goremageddon* (2003): the European gore line
5. Despised Icon – *The Ills of Modern Man* (2007): deathcore
6. Infant Annihilator – *The Elysian Grandeval Galèriarch* (2016): the modern extreme
