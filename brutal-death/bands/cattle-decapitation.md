# Cattle Decapitation

- **Base:** San Diego, California
- **Formed:** 1996
- **Key members:** Travis Ryan (vocals), Josh Elmore (guitar), Dave McGraw (drums)
- **Style:** Deathgrind, from goregrind to progressive, misanthropic death metal

## History
Cattle Decapitation started in the goregrind tradition of Carcass, with a twist: the band
members were vegetarian, and the gore was aimed at humans, as a protest against how people
treat animals. Early records like *To Serve Man* (2002) and *Humanure* (2004, Metal Blade),
whose cover was censored in many stores, were short, chaotic grindcore.

From *Karma.Bloody.Karma* (2006) and *The Harvest Floor* (2009) the songwriting grew more
ambitious. *Monolith of Inhumanity* (2012) was the breakthrough. Travis Ryan added an eerie,
high, half-clean "melodic" vocal on top of his growls and shrieks, and the themes widened to
humanity destroying the planet. *The Anthropocene Extinction* (2015), *Death Atlas* (2019) and
*Terrasite* (2023) carried that environmental-collapse concept further. They're one of the most
distinctive extreme metal bands from the US West Coast.
