# Lorna Shore

- **Base:** New Jersey
- **Formed:** 2009/2010 **(verify)**
- **Key members:** Adam De Micco (guitar), Austin Archey (drums), Will Ramos (vocals, from 2021); earlier vocalists include Tom Barber
- **Style:** Symphonic, blackened deathcore

## History
Lorna Shore spent their first decade as a solid deathcore band with frequent vocalist changes.
Tom Barber sang on *Immortal* (2020) before leaving for Chelsea Grin. New vocalist Will Ramos
debuted on the EP *...And I Return to Nothingness* (2021). The single "To the Hellfire" went viral
for his inhuman range of screams, growls and "pig squeals", and it made the band one of the
biggest names in modern extreme metal.

*Pain Remains* (2022), with its three-part closing suite "Pain Remains I–III", combined blast-beat
brutality with orchestral, cinematic arrangements. *I Feel the Everblack Festering Within Me*
(2025) **(verify)** followed.
