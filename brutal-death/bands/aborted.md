# Aborted

- **Base:** Waregem, Belgium
- **Formed:** 1995
- **Key member:** Sven "Svencho" de Caluwé (vocals, the only constant member)
- **Style:** Brutal death metal / deathgrind with goregrind roots

## History
Aborted began in the goregrind tradition of Carcass and grew into one of Europe's leading
brutal death metal bands. *Engineering the Dead* (2001) and *Goremageddon: The Saw and the
Carnage Done* (2003) made their name with relentless, precise blasting, medical and horror-movie
lyrics, and sample-filled intros. *The Archaic Abattoir* (2005) and *Strychnine.213* (2008) added
groove and some deathcore influence.

From *Global Flatline* (2012) on, with an international rotating lineup, every album has been a
horror-themed onslaught. *Retrogore* (2016) pays tribute to 80s horror, *TerrorVision* (2018) is
sci-fi horror, and *Maniacult* (2021) and *Vault of Horrors* (2024) **(verify)** followed.
