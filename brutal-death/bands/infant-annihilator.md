# Infant Annihilator

- **Base:** Hull, England
- **Formed:** 2012
- **Members:** Aaron Kitcher (drums), Eddie Pickard (guitar/bass), Dickie Allen (vocals, from 2016); Dan Watson (vocals on the debut)
- **Style:** Technical deathcore / brutal death metal

## History
Infant Annihilator started as a studio project. Drummer Aaron Kitcher and guitarist Eddie
Pickard released music and play-through videos online, and their YouTube channel got huge
view counts for the band's extreme speed, constant blasting, and deliberately outrageous lyrics.

The debut *The Palpable Leprosy of Pollution* (2012), with Dan Watson (later of Enterprise Earth)
on vocals, made their name. Dickie Allen's wide-ranging, theatrical vocals came in on *The
Elysian Grandeval Galèriarch* (2016), and *The Battle of Yaldabaoth* (2019) pushed further still.
For years the band rarely or never played live **(verify current live status)**, which added
to their mystique.
