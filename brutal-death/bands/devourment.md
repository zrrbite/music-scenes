# Devourment

- **Base:** Dallas, Texas
- **Formed:** 1995
- **Key members:** Ruben Rosas (vocals, later), Wayne Knupp (vocals on the debut), Mike Majewski (vocals, mid-period) **(verify lineup)**
- **Style:** Slam death metal

## History
Devourment took the slow, groovy "slam" sections of New York brutal death metal and made them
the point of the song. *Molesting the Decapitated* (1999) set out the slam template: ultra-low,
bouncing riffs, sudden blast-beat bursts, and gurgling, almost inhuman vocals. "Babykiller" is
one of the most famous songs in the style. The album spread through the tape-trading and early
internet underground and spawned a worldwide scene. The band has continued through many
lineup changes, with records like *Unleash the Carnivore* (2005) and *Conceived in Sewage* (2013).
