# Bring Me the Horizon

- **Base:** Sheffield, England
- **Formed:** 2004
- **Members:** Oli Sykes (vocals), Lee Malia (guitar), Matt Kean (bass), Matt Nicholls (drums); Jordan Fish (keyboards/production, 2012–2023)

## Why it's in this section
BMTH started as one of the UK's most notorious deathcore bands, and then became one of the
biggest rock acts in the world. They show how far deathcore's influence reached.

## History
*Count Your Blessings* (2006) was raw deathcore with breakdowns and screams. *Suicide Season*
(2008) and *There Is a Hell Believe Me I've Seen It. There Is a Heaven Let's Keep It a Secret.*
(2010) added electronics and melody. With keyboardist and producer Jordan Fish, *Sempiternal*
(2013), with "Shadow Moses" and "Can You Feel My Heart", made them arena headliners.

*That's the Spirit* (2015, with "Throne") went fully alt-rock, and *Amo* (2019) drew on pop and
electronica. The *Post Human* records, *Survival Horror* (2020) and *Nex Gen* (2024), brought back
much heavier metalcore and nu-metal sounds.
