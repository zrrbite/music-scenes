# Spiritbox

- **Base:** Victoria, British Columbia, Canada
- **Formed:** 2017
- **Members:** Courtney LaPlante (vocals), Mike Stringer (guitar), Josh Gilbert (bass, from 2023; ex-As I Lay Dying), Zev Rose (drums) **(verify current lineup)**
- **Style:** Djent / metalcore with atmospheric, electronic textures

## History
Courtney LaPlante and Mike Stringer, who are married, had both been in the chaotic Iwrestledabearonce.
They moved back to Stringer's home on Vancouver Island and started Spiritbox, which pairs
low-tuned djent riffs with ambient, electronic atmospheres and LaPlante's switches between
ethereal clean singing and vicious screams.

Singles like "Holy Roller" (2020) and "Circle with Me" went viral, and the debut *Eternal Blue*
(2021) was a breakthrough, earning Grammy nominations. *Tsunami Sea* (2025) **(verify)** followed.
They're one of the fastest-rising heavy bands of the 2020s.
