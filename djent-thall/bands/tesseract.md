# TesseracT

- **Base:** Milton Keynes, England
- **Formed:** 2003 as Acle Kahney's project; full band from about 2007
- **Members:** Acle Kahney (guitar, founder), Daniel Tompkins (vocals; left and returned), James Monteith (guitar), Amos Williams (bass), Jay Postones (drums); other vocalists Ashe O'Hara and Elliot Coleman

## History
Acle Kahney started TesseracT in the early 2000s, and it became the leading UK djent band. Their
sound pairs Meshuggah-style rhythmic riffing with spacious, ambient textures and soaring clean
vocals, which made them more melodic and atmospheric than most of their peers.

*One* (2011, Century Media), with Daniel Tompkins on vocals, was a landmark. Tompkins left, and
Ashe O'Hara sang on *Altered State* (2013), a fan favorite. Tompkins returned for *Polaris* (2015),
*Sonder* (2018) and *War of Being* (2023). Their clean-vocal, atmospheric approach influenced many
later bands, including Sleep Token.
