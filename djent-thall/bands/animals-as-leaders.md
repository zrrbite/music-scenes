# Animals as Leaders

- **Base:** Washington DC area
- **Formed:** 2007
- **Members:** Tosin Abasi (8-string guitar), Javier Reyes (8-string guitar), Matt Garstka (drums, from 2012)
- **Style:** Instrumental progressive metal / djent / jazz fusion

## History
Tosin Abasi had played in the metalcore band Reflux when a label offered him a solo record. He
recorded it with producer and co-writer Misha Mansoor of Periphery, and *Animals as Leaders*
(2009) showed off his 8-string playing: thumping and slapping techniques borrowed from bass,
jazz harmony, and djent rhythms. The project became a trio with Javier Reyes, and later drummer
Matt Garstka.

*Weightless* (2011), *The Joy of Motion* (2014), *The Madness of Many* (2016) and *Parrhesia* (2022)
made them one of the most influential guitar bands of their generation. Abasi also co-founded the
guitar brand Abasi Concepts.
