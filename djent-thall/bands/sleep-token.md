# Sleep Token

- **Base:** London, England
- **Formed:** 2016
- **Members:** anonymous and masked; the singer and songwriter is known as "Vessel", with the drummer "II" and others numbered
- **Style:** Djent riffs combined with R&B, pop, ambient and soul ballads

## History
Sleep Token appeared in 2016 with an elaborate mythology: anonymous masked members who say
their music is worship of an ancient deity called "Sleep". Musically they combine crushing,
djent-style riffs with piano ballads, R&B vocal runs and pop production, often within one song.
Drummer II has drawn particular praise.

*Sundowning* (2019) and *This Place Will Become Your Tomb* (2021) built a cult following. *Take Me
Back to Eden* (2023) went viral on social media, with songs like "The Summoning", and turned
them into one of the biggest new rock bands in the world. *Even in Arcadia* (2025) reached number
one in the US **(verify)**.
