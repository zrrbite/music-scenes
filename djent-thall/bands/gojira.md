# Gojira

- **Base:** Ondres / Bayonne, southwest France
- **Formed:** 1996 (as Godzilla; renamed Gojira in 2001)
- **Members:** Joe Duplantier (vocals/guitar), Mario Duplantier (drums), Christian Andreu (guitar), Jean-Michel Labadie (bass)

## History
Brothers Joe and Mario Duplantier grew up on the Basque coast and built Gojira around enormous,
polyrhythmic groove riffs, a Meshuggah and Morbid Angel influence, with signature pick-scrapes
and slides and Mario's powerful drumming. Their lyrics deal with nature, the environment and
spirituality.

*Terra Incognita* (2001) and *The Link* (2003) built their name in France. *From Mars to Sirius*
(2005), a concept album about saving the planet, featuring whales, made them internationally
known. *The Way of All Flesh* (2008), *L'Enfant Sauvage* (2012), *Magma* (2016) and *Fortitude*
(2021) took them to arena level. In 2024 they performed "Ah! Ça ira" at the opening ceremony of
the Paris Olympics from the Conciergerie, with opera singer Marina Viotti. The performance won a
Grammy in 2025 **(verify)**.
