# Mirar (MIRAR)

- **Base:** France / Norway (a French–Norwegian duo)
- **Members:** Léo Watremez (France, guitar), Marius Elfstedt (Norway, electronic production)
- **Style:** Instrumental tech-metal: thall / djent / progressive metal fused with electronic production and jazz and Baroque motifs
- **Website:** https://mirarband.com/ (store, Spotify link, Instagram/YouTube @mirar_officiel)

## History
Mirar began as a school jazz project and grew into a heavy, futuristic hybrid. **Léo Watremez**
is a guitarist with a background in jazz and Baroque music (Bach), and **Marius Elfstedt** is
an electronic music producer influenced by artists like Flume and Skrillex. The result combines
low, atmospheric thall/djent riffing and math-metal rhythms with synth-heavy soundscapes and
classical and jazz ideas.

Their production has reportedly involved **Buster Odeholm** (Humanity's Last Breath,
Vildhjarta) for mixing or mastering **(verify which releases)**. That's what places them in the
Swedish thall lineage even though neither member is Swedish.

## Where they fit
Mirar sits at the newest end of the djent → thall line: Meshuggah's rhythmic ideas, Vildhjarta's
dissonant atmosphere, and a production link to Humanity's Last Breath, combined with electronic
music and Baroque counterpoint. They're French like [Gojira](gojira.md), but they come from the
Swedish thall branch rather than the French death metal one.
