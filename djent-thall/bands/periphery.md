# Periphery

- **Base:** Washington DC area (Bethesda, Maryland)
- **Formed:** 2005
- **Members:** Misha Mansoor (guitar, founder, producer), Spencer Sotelo (vocals), Jake Bowen (guitar), Mark Holcomb (guitar), Matt Halpern (drums)

## History
Periphery started as Misha Mansoor's bedroom project. As "Bulb" he posted a huge number of
Meshuggah-inspired demos to online forums, and they're a big part of why "djent" became a scene.
Once the lineup settled, the self-titled debut (2010) was one of the first "djent" records on a
real label. *Periphery II: This Time It's Personal* (2012) is often seen as the genre's definitive
album: very technical, very heavy, but full of big pop choruses.

The *Juggernaut: Alpha / Omega* double album (2015), *Periphery III: Select Difficulty* (2016),
*Periphery IV: Hail Stan* (2019) and *Periphery V: Djent Is Not a Genre* (2023) followed. That
last title is a joke about the label they helped create. Mansoor also co-founded the guitar
gear company Horizon Devices.
