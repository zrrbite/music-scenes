# Djent & Thall

This one is organized by style rather than place: Meshuggah's rhythmic revolution and everything
that grew from it worldwide. That includes the internet-born djent of the late 2000s, Sweden's
darker "thall", and the arena-sized bands of today.

## History

### The source (1990s – 2000s)
**[Meshuggah](../swedish-metal/bands/meshuggah.md)** built riffs out of long, odd-numbered rhythmic
cycles played against a steady 4/4, on 7- and later 8-string guitars with a tight, palm-muted
tone. People started imitating the sound of that chug with the made-up word "djent". In France,
**[Gojira](bands/gojira.md)** developed their own polyrhythmic, crushing groove in parallel,
with environmental themes. They're more death metal than djent, but they're a constant
reference point for the same audience.

### Djent goes online (2005 – 2012)
In the late 2000s cheap home recording and amp-simulation software let guitarists produce
Meshuggah-style tones in their bedrooms. Online forums and early YouTube became the scene.
Misha Mansoor ("Bulb") posted hundreds of demos that became **[Periphery](bands/periphery.md)**,
Tosin Abasi's 8-string virtuosity became **[Animals as Leaders](bands/animals-as-leaders.md)**
(produced by Mansoor), and in England **[TesseracT](bands/tesseract.md)** added soaring clean
vocals and ambient space. "Djent" went from joke to genre name between about 2009 and 2012.

### Thall (2011 – now)
Sweden's **[Vildhjarta](../swedish-metal/bands/vildhjarta.md)** pushed the style into something
eerier and more dissonant and called it "thall". **[Humanity's Last
Breath](../swedish-metal/bands/humanitys-last-breath.md)** made it crushingly heavy, and producer
Buster Odeholm became central to the sound. Newer acts like **[Mirar](bands/mirar.md)** combine thall
with electronic production and classical ideas.

### The mainstream (2020s)
Djent's rhythms and tones went into the biggest heavy bands of the 2020s. Canada's
**[Spiritbox](bands/spiritbox.md)** mixed them with atmospheric metalcore and Courtney LaPlante's
range. London's anonymous **[Sleep Token](bands/sleep-token.md)** blended them with R&B, pop and
ballads, went viral with *Take Me Back to Eden* (2023), and reached the top of the charts. Gojira
played the opening ceremony of the Paris 2024 Olympics.
