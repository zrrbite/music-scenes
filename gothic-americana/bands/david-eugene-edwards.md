# David Eugene Edwards (solo)

- **Base:** Denver, Colorado
- **Solo debut:** *Hyacinth* (2023, Sargent House)
- **Also:** [The Denver Gentlemen](the-denver-gentlemen.md) **(verify)**, [16 Horsepower](16-horsepower.md), [Wovenhand](wovenhand.md)

## Who he is
Edwards grew up in Colorado. His grandfather was a Church of the Nazarene preacher, and he
often traveled with him as a child. That upbringing (revival meetings, scripture, the
fire-and-brimstone sermon) is the source of nearly everything in his music. He plays guitar,
banjo, bandoneon and mandolin. His voice moves between a whisper, a chant and a strained
howl, and on stage he performs with a trance-like physical intensity.

## The solo turn
Wovenhand itself started as a solo project in 2001, but by the 2010s it had become a full,
heavy rock band. *Hyacinth* (2023), released on Sargent House, the label home of Chelsea Wolfe
and Emma Ruth Rundle, was the first record under his own name. It strips the sound back to
Edwards alone, with guitar and layered drones and textures, closer to the sparse, hypnotic
side of early Wovenhand than to its heavy late period **(verify production details)**.

He has toured it as a solo performer: one man, his instruments and loops, building a dense,
ritual-like sound in the room.
