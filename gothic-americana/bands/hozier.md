# Hozier

- **Base:** Bray, County Wicklow, Ireland
- **Active:** 2013–now
- **Real name:** Andrew Hozier-Byrne

## History
Hozier studied music at Trinity College Dublin and sang with the Irish choral group Anúna. His
music mixes blues, soul, gospel and Irish folk with dark, gothic and religious imagery. "Take Me
to Church" (2013) became a global hit, and his self-titled debut (2014) followed.

*Wasteland, Baby!* (2019) and *Unreal Unearth* (2023), loosely structured around the nine circles
of Dante's *Inferno*, kept that mix of sensuality, myth and darkness. "Too Sweet" (2024) became his
first US number one.

## Why it's in this section
Hozier is the most popular modern artist working the Irish gothic-folk vein, with the same Old
World mix of hymn, ballad and darkness as [Lankum](lankum.md), in a mainstream soul/blues form.
