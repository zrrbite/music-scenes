# Tom Waits

- **Base:** Southern California, later Northern California
- **Active:** 1971–now
- **Key collaborator:** Kathleen Brennan (wife and co-writer since the 1980s)

## History
Tom Waits began in the 70s as a piano-bar balladeer of late-night LA, on *Closing Time* (1973) and
*Small Change* (1976), with a voice that kept getting rougher. With Kathleen Brennan he reinvented
himself on *Swordfishtrombones* (1983) and *Rain Dogs* (1985): junkyard percussion, marimbas, pump
organs, carnival and Tin Pan Alley ghosts, and songs full of drifters, freaks and murder.

*Bone Machine* (1992) won a Grammy. *Mule Variations* (1999) came out on **Anti-**, the sister label
of Epitaph, which ties him to Brett Gurewitz and [California punk](../../california-punk/README.md).
*Alice* and *Blood Money* (2002) and *Bad as Me* (2011) followed. He's also an actor. His
dark American storytelling is an ancestor of everything in this section.
