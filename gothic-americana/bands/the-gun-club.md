# The Gun Club

- **Base:** Los Angeles
- **Active:** 1979–1996
- **Key members:** Jeffrey Lee Pierce (vocals, d. 1996), Kid Congo Powers (guitar)

## History
Jeffrey Lee Pierce, a blues and reggae obsessive who ran the Blondie fan club, formed The Gun
Club in the LA punk scene. *Fire of Love* (1981) fused punk speed with Delta blues, voodoo
imagery and Pierce's wailing voice. "Sex Beat" and the cover of Robert Johnson's "Preaching the
Blues" are classics. It's often named as a foundation of "cowpunk", punk blues and gothic Americana.

*Miami* (1982) and *The Las Vegas Story* (1984) followed. Through lineup changes and Pierce's
health and addiction problems, the band went on until his death in 1996. Guitarist Kid Congo
Powers also played with The Cramps and Nick Cave & the Bad Seeds.
