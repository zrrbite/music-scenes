# 16 Horsepower

- **Base:** Denver, Colorado
- **Active:** 1992–2005
- **Members:** David Eugene Edwards (vocals, banjo, bandoneon, guitar), Pascal Humbert (bass), Jean-Yves Tola (drums), Steve Taylor / Jeffrey-Paul Norlander (guitar, at different times) **(verify lineup)**

## History
David Eugene Edwards grew up in Colorado. His grandfather was a Church of the Nazarene
preacher, and the fire-and-brimstone imagery of that upbringing runs through everything he's
done. 16 Horsepower took Appalachian folk, old-time country and gospel and played them with
punk intensity and a sense of spiritual dread. Edwards' voice was a strained, almost frightened howl.

They signed to A&M, and *Sackcloth 'n' Ashes* (1996) set out their sound with songs like "Black
Soul Choir". *Low Estate* (1997) was more polished, and *Secret South* (2000) and the traditional-heavy
*Folklore* (2002) came out on the European label Glitterhouse. The band never broke through
in the US, but it built a devoted following in Europe, especially France and the Benelux countries.

They broke up in 2005, citing political and spiritual differences. By then Edwards was
concentrating on [Wovenhand](wovenhand.md).
