# The Drones

- **Base:** formed in Perth, later based in Melbourne, Australia
- **Active:** 1997–2016 (inactive since) **(verify)**
- **Key members:** Gareth Liddiard (vocals/guitar, songwriter), Fiona Kitschin (bass), Rui Pereira (guitar, early), Dan Luscombe (guitar, from mid-2000s), Mike Noga (drums, mid-2000s to early 2010s) **(verify lineup years)**
- **Style:** Noisy, swampy blues-punk and Australian gothic storytelling

## History
Gareth Liddiard and Rui Pereira started the Drones in Perth in 1997 and moved to Melbourne
around the turn of the millennium. Their sound was ragged, feedback-heavy blues rock, and
Liddiard's long, literary, sardonic lyrics were sung in a voice that cracks and snarls. He
writes about Australian history, colonial violence, addiction and small-town despair the way
Nick Cave writes about the imagined American South.

*Here Come the Lies* (2002) introduced them. *Wait Long by the River and the Bodies of Your
Enemies Will Float By* (2005) was their breakthrough and won the first Australian Music Prize.
*Gala Mill* (2006) was recorded in an old farmhouse in Tasmania and soaked up its colonial
history. *Havilah* (2008), which includes "Your Acting's Like the End of the World", and *I See
Seaweed* (2013) cemented them as one of the most acclaimed Australian bands of their era.
"Shark Fin Blues" (from *Wait Long*) is often named among the best Australian songs ever
written **(verify)**.

*Feelin Kinda Free* (2016) was their most experimental record, with more electronics and
fractured songs. After it, Liddiard and Kitschin formed **Tropical Fuck Storm**, which carries
the same spirit into wilder, noisier territory. Former drummer Mike Noga died in 2020 **(verify)**.
