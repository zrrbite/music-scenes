# The Denver Gentlemen

- **Base:** Denver, Colorado
- **Active:** late 1980s – 1990s **(verify)**
- **Members:** included David Eugene Edwards, Slim Cessna and Jeffrey-Paul Norlander **(verify)**

## History
A Denver group playing dark, cabaret-tinged folk and old-time music. It's remembered mostly
as the starting point of the Denver Sound: members went on to form
[16 Horsepower](16-horsepower.md) and [Slim Cessna's Auto Club](slim-cessnas-auto-club.md).
