# Wovenhand

- **Base:** Denver, Colorado
- **Active:** 2001–now
- **Key member:** David Eugene Edwards; later lineups include Chuck French (guitar), Neil Keener (bass) and Ordy Garrison (drums) **(verify)**

## History
Wovenhand began as David Eugene Edwards' solo outlet while 16 Horsepower was still going. The
self-titled debut (2002) was quieter and more mystical than 16 Horsepower, with Native
American and Eastern European textures alongside the Appalachian roots. *Blush Music* (2003)
was written for a dance performance by Wim Vandekeybus' company Ultima Vez.

*Consider the Birds* (2004), *Mosaic* (2006) and *Ten Stones* (2008) built a hypnotic, tribal
and increasingly heavy sound. *The Threshingfloor* (2010) and *The Laughing Stalk* (2012)
turned up the volume. *Refractory Obdurate* (2014), released on the hardcore label Deathwish,
and *Star Treatment* (2016) brought them fully into heavy-music circles. Wovenhand toured with
post-metal and metal bands and won over a new audience there.
