# Nick Cave & the Bad Seeds

- **Base:** formed in London/Berlin by Australian expatriates; later based in Brighton, UK
- **Active:** 1983–now
- **Key members:** Nick Cave (vocals), Mick Harvey (to 2009), Blixa Bargeld (to 2003), Warren Ellis (since 1990s)

## History
After his chaotic post-punk band The Birthday Party ended, Nick Cave formed the Bad Seeds.
Early albums like *From Her to Eternity* (1984) and *Your Funeral... My Trial* (1986) were steeped
in a fevered, imagined American South of preachers, killers and floods. *Tender Prey* (1988)
opened with "The Mercy Seat".

*Let Love In* (1994), with "Red Right Hand", and *Murder Ballads* (1996), a whole album of
traditional and original killing songs, are the records most connected to gothic Americana.
Cave later moved toward intimate piano ballads (*The Boatman's Call*, 1997) and, with Warren
Ellis, ambient grief records (*Skeleton Tree*, 2016, *Ghosteen*, 2019). He's a major influence
on David Eugene Edwards' generation.
