# Slim Cessna's Auto Club

- **Base:** Denver, Colorado
- **Active:** 1992–now
- **Key members:** Slim Cessna (vocals), Jay Munly (vocals, banjo), Lord Dwight Pentacost (steel guitar) **(verify)**

## History
Slim Cessna's Auto Club plays gothic country with punk energy and the feel of a revival
meeting: two lead singers trading lines, pedal steel and banjo, and lyrics full of sin,
salvation and small-town Western violence. Their live shows are famously theatrical.
Jay Munly's songwriting, often set in his imaginary "Lupercalia" mythology, is a big part
of their later sound. *Cipher* (2008) and *Unentitled* (2011) are good entry points **(verify years)**.
Jello Biafra's label Alternative Tentacles released several of their records.
